❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a sleep cycle?
A sleep cycle lasts ~90 min: light sleep → deep sleep → REM dream sleep. Waking between cycles = refreshed feeling.
How many cycles needed?
Adults need 5-6 cycles (7.5-9 hrs). Our calculator shows 4-6 optimal options.
What is Brahma Muhurta?
Brahma Muhurta = 1.5 hrs before sunrise (~4:30-5:30 AM). Ayurveda's ideal wake time for mental clarity.
What are the 4 chronotypes?
🦁 Lion (early), 🐻 Bear (sun-aligned), 🐺 Wolf (night owl), 🐬 Dolphin (light sleeper). Each has unique ideal schedules.
