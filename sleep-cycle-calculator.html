    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I manage sleep for night shift work in India?

    For night shift (10 PM – 7 AM), sleep during the day in a dark room (blackout curtains + eye mask), maintain a consistent schedule even on off days, avoid caffeine after 3 AM, and prioritize 7-8 hours. Shift-work sleep disorder is common in Indian IT/BPO — fixed shifts are better than rotating. If you cannot sleep 1 hour after shift end, seek a sleep specialist.

    Why is sleep difficult in Indian summer?

    In Indian summers (Apr–Jun), room temperatures >30°C disrupt sleep onset and reduce deep sleep. Optimal sleep temperature is 18–22°C, which is often not achievable without AC. Strategies: cool shower before bed, cotton/linen bedding, fan + wet towel on forehead, light cotton clothes, hydrate. Avoid alcohol — it worsens heat-related dehydration during sleep.

    Do Ayurvedic sleep aids work?

    Some have evidence: Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) 300–600 mg at night reduces cortisol and improves sleep quality; Brahmi (Bacopa monnieri) 300 mg supports calm; Jatamansi (Nardostachys jatamansi) is a traditional sedative. Warm milk with nutmeg has tryptophan + sedative effect. These are supportive — not replacements for medical treatment. Avoid mixing with prescription sedatives.

    How much sleep do Indian teenagers need?

    Per AASM/ICMR: teens 14–17 need 8–10 hours per night. Indian teens often sleep less due to early school (7 AM), tuitions, and late-night screens. Target 9 hours = 6 complete cycles. Napping 20–30 min after school helps without disrupting night sleep.

    How do I fix sleep debt from late-night OTT/WhatsApp?

    Blue light and stimulating content delay melatonin by 1–2 hours. Fix: no screens 60 min before bed, use Night Shift/warm display after 8 PM, keep phone outside the bedroom, charge overnight in another room. If you must scroll, use an e-reader with warm light. Add 1 hour sleep per night for 5–7 nights to repay debt.

    What is a sleep cycle?

    A sleep cycle is a ~90-minute pattern: N1 (light), N2 (light), N3 (deep), REM (dreaming). Adults complete 4–6 cycles per night. Waking mid-cycle (especially during deep N3) causes sleep inertia — grogginess lasting up to 30 min.

    Is it better to sleep 6 hours or 7.5 hours?

    7.5 hours (5 complete cycles) is far better than 6 hours (4 incomplete cycles with likely mid-deep-sleep wake-up). It's not just duration — it's about completing full 90-minute cycles so you wake up at the natural end of a cycle.

    How do I fix sleep debt?

    You cannot fix sleep debt in one night. Add 1–1.5 hours per night for 3–7 nights until repaid. Maintain consistent bedtime, avoid caffeine after 2 PM, and get morning sunlight within 30 min of waking. Napping 20 min is safe; longer naps worsen sleep debt.
