❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a sleep cycle?
A sleep cycle lasts about 90 minutes and includes light sleep, deep sleep, and REM sleep. Waking up between cycles helps you feel refreshed.
How many cycles do I need?
Most adults need 5-6 complete cycles (7.5-9 hours). The calculator shows 4-6 cycle options.
What is sleep debt?
Sleep debt is the cumulative effect of not getting enough sleep. A debt of 10+ hours requires a week of extended sleep to recover.
What's my chronotype?
Chronotype is your natural preference for sleeping/waking times. Lions wake early, wolves wake late, bears follow the sun, dolphins are light sleepers.
