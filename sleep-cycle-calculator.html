❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a sleep cycle?
A sleep cycle lasts ~90 minutes and includes light sleep, deep sleep, and REM (dream) sleep. Waking up between cycles helps you feel refreshed instead of groggy.
How many sleep cycles do I need?
Most adults need 5-6 complete cycles (7.5-9 hours). Our calculator shows 4-6 cycle options for both bedtime and wake-time planning.
What is Brahma Muhurta in Ayurveda?
Brahma Muhurta is the auspicious time 1.5 hours before sunrise. Ayurveda recommends waking during this time for optimal physical and mental health.
What are the 4 chronotypes?
Lion (early bird, wake 5-6 AM), Bear (follows sun, wake 7-8 AM), Wolf (night owl, wake 9-10 AM), Dolphin (light sleeper, irregular pattern).
Is my sleep data private?
Yes! All calculations happen in your browser. We never store, log, or transmit any of your personal health data. 100% private and anonymous.
How does the sleep debt tracker work?
Sleep debt is calculated as (Target sleep - Average sleep) × 7 days. The progress bar shows how much recovery is needed, and we provide a personalized recovery plan.
