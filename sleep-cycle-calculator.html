❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a sleep cycle?
A sleep cycle lasts about 90 minutes and includes light sleep, deep sleep, and REM sleep. Waking up between cycles helps you feel refreshed.
How many cycles do I need?
Most adults need 5-6 complete cycles (7.5-9 hours). The calculator shows 4-6 cycle options.
What is Brahma Muhurta?
Brahma Muhurta is the auspicious time 1.5 hours before sunrise (around 4:30-5:30 AM). Ayurveda recommends waking during this time for optimal health.
What's my Ayurvedic Dosha?
Vata types sleep lightly, Pitta moderately, Kapha deeply. Knowing your dosha helps optimize sleep habits.
