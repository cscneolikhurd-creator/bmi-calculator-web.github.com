Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Mifflin-St Jeor equation?

The most accurate formula for Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR). Men: (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) − (5 × age) + 5. Women: same but − 161. Accurate within ±10% for most people.

What is TDEE vs BMR?

BMR = calories burned at complete rest. TDEE = BMR × activity factor — the total calories you actually burn per day including exercise and daily movement.

How many calories should an Indian eat to lose weight?

A safe deficit is 300-500 kcal below TDEE. For most Indian adults, that's 1400-1800 kcal/day for women and 1600-2200 for men. Never go below 1200 kcal (women) or 1500 kcal (men) without medical supervision.

What are good Indian protein sources?

Vegetarian: paneer (18g/100g), soya chunks (52g/100g), dal (9g/cooked cup), rajma, chole, curd (11g/100g), Greek yogurt.
Eggetarian: + eggs (6g each).
Non-veg: + chicken breast (31g/100g), fish (22g/100g), mutton (25g/100g).

How much protein do Indians need per kg?

General health: 0.8-1.0 g/kg. Weight loss / muscle gain: 1.6-2.2 g/kg. Endurance athletes: 1.2-1.4 g/kg. For a 70kg Indian training for fat loss, that's 112-154g protein/day. Traditional Indian diets are often carb-heavy and protein-light — this is the #1 gap.

What is a good Indian meal plan for weight loss?

Example 1600 kcal veg day: Breakfast — 2 moong dal chillas + curd; Snack — 100g paneer + green tea; Lunch — 1 roti + 100g paneer sabzi + dal + salad; Snack — buttermilk + roasted chana; Dinner — paneer bhurji + 1 roti + sabzi. Protein ~85g, Carbs ~140g, Fat ~55g.

How accurate is this calculator?

Mifflin-St Jeor: ±10%. Katch-McArdle (with body fat %): ±5-8%. Harris-Benedict (revised): ±12%. Individual metabolism varies. Use as a starting point and adjust based on 2-4 week weight trends.

Do I need to count calories to lose weight?

Not strictly, but tracking improves outcomes by 30-40%. The calorie deficit is what matters — tracking is just the measurement tool.
