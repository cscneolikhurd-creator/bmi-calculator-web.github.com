Frequently Asked Questions

What is vascular age?
Vascular age estimates the biological age of your arteries based on cardiovascular risk factors. If it's higher than your actual age, your arteries are aging faster, increasing your risk of heart attack and stroke.
What is pulse wave velocity (PWV)?
PWV measures how fast blood pressure waves travel through your arteries. Lower values (<7 m/s) indicate healthy, flexible arteries. Higher values suggest stiffer arteries and increased cardiovascular risk.
Can vascular age be reversed?
Yes! Studies show that lifestyle changes can reverse vascular aging. Regular exercise, Mediterranean/DASH diet, blood pressure control, quitting smoking, and stress management can reduce vascular age by 5-10 years.
How accurate is this calculator?
Based on the Framingham Heart Study with over 70 years of cardiovascular research data — the gold standard worldwide. Provides a research-based estimate of vascular health.
Is my data private?
Yes! All calculations happen entirely in your browser. We never store, log, or transmit any of your personal health data to any server. 100% private and anonymous.
How often should I check my vascular age?
For most adults, checking every 6-12 months is sufficient. If you're actively making lifestyle changes, every 3-4 months can help track progress. Always consult your doctor.
