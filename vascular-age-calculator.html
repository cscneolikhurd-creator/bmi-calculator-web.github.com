❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is vascular age?
Vascular age estimates the biological age of your arteries based on risk factors. If higher than your actual age, your arteries are aging faster, increasing cardiovascular risk.
What is pulse wave velocity?
PWV measures arterial stiffness. Lower values ( <7 m/s) indicate flexible, healthy arteries. Higher values suggest stiffer arteries and increased risk.
Can vascular age be reversed?
Yes! Regular exercise, healthy diet, blood pressure control, quitting smoking, and stress management can reverse vascular aging by 5-10 years.
