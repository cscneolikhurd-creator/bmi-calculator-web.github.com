Frequently Asked Questions

Can vegetarians do keto in India?

Yes, but it's harder. Vegetarian keto relies on paneer (18g protein/100g), ghee, coconut oil, cheese, cream, soya chunks (52g/100g), tofu, and low-carb veggies. Avoid dal, rice, roti, and most Indian sabzi staples. Target 20–25g net carbs/day. Consider eggetarian or pescatarian for easier keto.

What Indian foods should I avoid on keto?

Avoid: Dal (all types ~20g carbs/cup), rice (~45g/cup), roti/chapati (~15g each), poha, upma, idli, dosa, potato, mithai, chai with sugar, fruit (except 1/2 cup berries), packaged namkeen, biscuits. Limit: onions, tomatoes (in moderation), milk (12g carbs/cup), cashews (high carb).

What are the best Indian keto foods?

Paneer (18g protein/100g, 3g carbs), ghee, coconut oil, mustard oil, eggs, chicken, fish, mutton, cauliflower (rice substitute), palak (spinach), methi, broccoli, mushrooms, tofu, soya chunks, almonds, walnuts, avocado, cheese, cream, dark chocolate 85%+.

Is keto safe for Indians with diabetes?

Keto can be effective for type 2 diabetes and pre-diabetes in Indians (high carb sensitivity). BUT: consult your diabetologist before starting — insulin/sulfonylurea doses must be reduced, or you risk hypoglycemia. Type 1 diabetics need close supervision. NOT safe for those with kidney disease, liver disease, or pancreatitis.

What is keto flu and how do I avoid it?

Keto flu includes headaches, fatigue, brain fog in week 1. It's caused by electrolyte loss. Prevent with: Sodium 3000–5000mg (add salt, bone broth), Potassium 3000–4700mg (spinach, avocado), Magnesium 300–500mg (supplement, nuts). Also drink 3–4L water daily.

What are net carbs?

Net carbs = Total carbs − Fiber − Half of sugar alcohols. Fiber isn't digested, so it doesn't spike blood sugar. Example: 10g carbs − 5g fiber = 5g net carbs. This is the number to track on keto.

What are the four keto protocols?

1) Standard Keto (75% fat, 20% protein, 5% carbs) — most common. 2) High Protein Keto (65/30/5) — for muscle preservation. 3) Therapeutic Keto (90/8/2) — for epilepsy/medical. 4) Targeted Keto (70/25/5) — carbs timed around workouts.

How long does it take to enter ketosis?

Most people enter ketosis in 2–7 days of strict keto (<20g net carbs). Athletes may enter in 24–48 hours. Confirmed by blood ketones of 0.5–3.0 mmol/L. Urine strips lose accuracy after the first week — use a blood meter for reliability.

Medical Disclaimer

This keto calculator is for educational and informational purposes only. The ketogenic diet is a medical nutrition therapy that should be followed under professional supervision, especially for therapeutic use (epilepsy, type 2 diabetes, PCOS).

This tool is not a substitute for professional medical advice, registered dietitian consultation, or treatment. Keto is NOT recommended for: pregnant/breastfeeding women, people with kidney disease, type 1 diabetics (without medical supervision), pancreatitis, liver disease, gallbladder disease, or history of eating disorders.

Consult a physician and registered dietitian before starting keto, especially if you take medications (insulin, BP, thyroid) — dosages often need adjustment. Long-term effects (>2 years) are not well studied.
