Frequently Asked Questions

How many carbs should I eat on keto?

Standard keto requires 20-50g of net carbs per day, with 20-25g being the most common target for beginners. Net carbs = Total carbs − Fiber. Athletes may tolerate up to 50g. Therapeutic keto (for epilepsy) requires stricter 10-15g.

What are the four types of keto diets?

1) Standard Keto (75% fat, 20% protein, 5% carbs) — most common.
2) High Protein Keto (65/30/5) — for muscle preservation.
3) Therapeutic Keto (90/8/2) — for epilepsy/medical use.
4) Targeted Keto (70/25/5) — carbs timed around workouts.

How long does it take to enter ketosis?

Most people enter ketosis in 2-7 days of strict keto (<20g net carbs). Athletes and people with higher metabolism may enter in 24-48 hours. Ketosis is confirmed by blood ketones of 0.5-3.0 mmol/L.

What is keto flu and how do I avoid it?

Keto flu includes headaches, fatigue, and brain fog during the first week. It's caused by electrolyte loss. Prevent it with: Sodium 3000-5000mg, Potassium 3000-4700mg, Magnesium 300-500mg daily. Drink bone broth, add salt to food, eat leafy greens.

Is keto safe for everyone?

Keto is NOT recommended for: pregnant/breastfeeding women, people with kidney disease, type 1 diabetics (without supervision), pancreatitis, liver disease, or eating disorders. Consult a doctor before starting. Long-term effects (>2 years) are not well studied.

What are net carbs?

Net carbs = Total carbohydrates − Dietary fiber − Half of sugar alcohols. Fiber isn't digested, so it doesn't spike blood sugar. Example: 10g carbs − 5g fiber = 5g net carbs. This is the number to track on keto.
