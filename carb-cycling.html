Frequently Asked Questions

What is carb cycling?

Carb cycling alternates between high-carb, low-carb, and rest days through the week. High-carb days fuel intense workouts and replenish glycogen; low-carb days enhance fat oxidation; rest days promote recovery.

How many high and low carb days per week?

Standard: 3 high, 2 low, 2 rest
Aggressive Cut: 2 high, 3 low, 2 rest
Maintenance: 4 high, 2 low, 1 rest
Keto Cycle: 1 high, 2 low, 4 rest
Align high-carb days with your hardest workouts.

What should Indians eat on high carb days?

Veg: poha, idli with sambar, brown rice, millet roti, oats with banana, rajma chawal, khichdi, dates.
Non-veg: chicken + rice, fish curry + roti.
Vegan: soya chunks + rice, tofu with rice.
Choose whole-food carbs over refined.

What should Indians eat on low carb days?

Veg: paneer bhurji, dal with small ghee, sabzi, curd, sprouts chaat, cucumber salad, soya chunks, tofu.
Non-veg: grilled chicken + sabzi, tandoori chicken, fish tikka.
Keep protein 1.8–2.2 g/kg.

Can I do carb cycling as a vegetarian?

Yes. High-carb days: rajma, chole, khichdi, poha, millet roti, brown rice. Low-carb days: paneer, tofu, soya chunks, dal, curd, sprouts. Protein target stays the same (1.6–2.2 g/kg) from plant sources.

Is carb cycling better than a standard diet?

Research shows carb cycling and standard calorie restriction produce similar fat loss when calories and protein are matched. Carb cycling may improve workout performance on high days and adherence for some people. It's not required for fat loss.

How long until carb cycling shows results?

Fat loss results typically show in 2–4 weeks. Initial weight loss in week 1 is mostly water (glycogen depletion). Muscle preservation and performance improvements may appear within 3–6 weeks.

Why does my low-carb day have more fat than high-carb day?

Low-carb days compensate for reduced carbs with higher fat to maintain calorie targets. Fat is used for fuel when carbs are low. High-carb days keep fat moderate. This is standard carb cycling logic.

This carb cycling planner is for educational and informational purposes only. Carb cycling is a nutrition strategy — not a medical treatment. Results vary based on genetics, metabolic health, training intensity, and adherence.
