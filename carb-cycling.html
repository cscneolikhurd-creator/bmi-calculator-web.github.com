How Carb Cycling Works

🔵 High Carb Days: Fuel intense workouts, replenish glycogen, boost metabolism

🔴 Rest/No Carb Days: Recovery, enhance fat oxidation, improve metabolic flexibility

Food Suggestions by Day Type

🔵 High Carb Day Foods

  • Brown rice, quinoa, oats
  • Sweet potatoes, yams
  • Whole grain pasta, bread
  • Bananas, dates, mangoes
  • Lean chicken, turkey
  • Low-fat dairy

🟡 Low Carb Day Foods

  • Leafy greens, broccoli
  • Berries, green apples
  • Eggs, egg whites
  • Fish, seafood
  • Nuts, seeds (moderate)
  • Cottage cheese

🔴 Rest Day Foods

  • Fatty fish (salmon)
  • Avocados, olives
  • Beef, lamb, pork
  • Cheese, butter, cream
  • Above-ground vegetables
  • Bone broth
