Frequently Asked Questions

What is Blood Alcohol Content (BAC)?

Blood Alcohol Content (BAC) is the percentage of alcohol in your bloodstream. A BAC of 0.08% means 0.08 grams of alcohol per 100 mL of blood. In most countries, 0.08% is the legal driving limit.

How accurate is the Widmark formula?

The Widmark formula is accurate within ±20% for most people. It doesn't account for liver health, food intake, medications, or genetic factors. Use it for educational purposes only — never to decide whether to drive.

What is the legal BAC limit in the US?

The legal BAC limit for driving in all 50 US states is 0.08%. Commercial drivers have a stricter limit of 0.04%, and drivers under 21 have zero tolerance (0.00–0.02%).

How long does it take to sober up?

The average person metabolizes alcohol at ~0.015% BAC per hour. No amount of coffee, food, or cold showers will speed this up — only time works.

What is a standard drink?

In the US, a standard drink = 14g pure alcohol (12 oz beer @5%, 5 oz wine @12%, or 1.5 oz spirits @40%). UK uses 8g, Australia uses 10g — standards differ by country.

Medical Disclaimer

This BAC calculator is for educational purposes only and uses the Widmark formula, which provides estimates within ±20% accuracy. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, a breathalyzer, or blood test. Individual factors (liver health, medications, food, genetics) significantly affect actual BAC. Never use this tool to decide whether to drive. If you've been drinking, don't drive. If you suspect alcohol poisoning, call emergency services immediately.
