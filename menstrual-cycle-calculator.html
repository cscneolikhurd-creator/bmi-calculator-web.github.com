❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best Ayurvedic remedies for period pain?
Evidence-supported Indian remedies: jeera (cumin) water — anti-spasmodic; ajwain (carom seeds) — relieves cramping; saunf (fennel) tea — reduces bloating; methi seeds — may reduce cramps; haldi doodh with black pepper — anti-inflammatory; til oil massage on lower abdomen. Use as supportive, not replacement for medical care.
How common is PCOS in India?
PCOS/PCOD affects 1 in 5 Indian women — 2-3x higher than Western populations. Signs: irregular periods, acne, excess facial hair, weight gain, difficulty conceiving. Diagnosis needs Rotterdam criteria (2 of 3): irregular ovulation, high androgens, polycystic ovaries. Ask for LH, FSH, testosterone, AMH + pelvic ultrasound.
How is ovulation calculated?
Ovulation typically occurs 14 days before your next period. For a 28-day cycle, ovulation is on day 14. Calendar-based prediction has ±2-3 day accuracy — use basal body temperature (BBT) or ovulation predictor kits (OPK) for better precision.
Why is my period late or irregular?
Common causes: stress, weight changes, PCOS, thyroid disorders (hypothyroidism is common in India), excessive exercise, medications (contraceptive pill changes, emergency contraception), recent travel, perimenopause (45+), postpartum. If cycles are consistently <21 or >35 days, or vary >9 days, consult a gynecologist.
When should I see a doctor for severe symptoms?
Seek urgent medical care for: (1) Soaking 1 pad/tampon every 1-2 hours, (2) Bleeding >7 days, (3) Blood clots larger than a rupee coin, (4) Severe pain not relieved by OTC painkillers, (5) Fainting or dizziness, (6) Fever with pelvic pain, (7) Missed periods for 3+ months (not pregnant), (8) Sudden severe pelvic pain. India: call 108 for ambulance or visit nearest government hospital.
How does the contraceptive pill affect my cycle?
The combined oral contraceptive pill makes withdrawal bleeding predictable, but it's NOT a real period — ovulation is suppressed. Progestin-only pills, IUDs, and implants can cause irregular spotting or amenorrhea. Our calculator works best for natural cycles — if on hormonal contraception, talk to your gynecologist about expected bleeding patterns.
What cycle length is normal?
Normal cycle length is 21-35 days (28 average). Variations of 7-9 days per year are normal. Persistent irregularity or cycles outside this range warrant medical evaluation, especially for PCOS or thyroid concerns.
Can I use this for contraception?
NO. This tool is for cycle awareness only. Calendar-based methods have 12-24% typical-use failure rate — many unintended pregnancies. Use proper contraception (condoms, pill, IUD, implant) and consult a gynecologist. Fertility awareness methods require BBT tracking + cervical mucus + strict discipline.

Medical Disclaimer

This menstrual cycle calculator is for educational and informational purposes only. Cycle predictions are estimates based on the calendar method — they do not account for irregular cycles, PCOS, thyroid conditions, perimenopause, postpartum changes, or hormonal contraception.

This tool is not a substitute for professional medical advice, contraception, or diagnosis. Do not use this tracker as birth control. For family planning or irregular cycle concerns, consult a gynecologist or reproductive health specialist.

Seek urgent medical care if you experience: heavy bleeding (soaking 1 pad/hour), bleeding >7 days, blood clots larger than a rupee coin, severe pain not relieved by OTC medication, fainting, or fever with pelvic pain. In India: call 108 or visit nearest government hospital.

Ayurvedic remedies are presented as supportive options — not replacements for medical treatment. Do not delay evidence-based medical care in favor of traditional remedies.
