❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is ovulation calculated?
Ovulation typically occurs 14 days before your next period. For a 28-day cycle, ovulation is on day 14. Our calculator uses your specific cycle length.
What are Ayurvedic period care tips?
Ayurveda recommends warm foods, ginger tea, sesame oil massage, and rest during periods to balance Vata dosha and reduce discomfort.
How accurate is PMS prediction?
Predictions are based on cycle phase and common symptoms. Individual experiences may vary. Tracking your own symptoms over 2-3 cycles improves accuracy.
Is my period data private?
Yes! All calculations happen in your browser. We never store, log, or transmit any of your personal health data. 100% private.
Can I use this for contraception?
NO. This tool is for cycle awareness only. Calendar-based methods have a 12-24% typical-use failure rate. Use proper contraception and consult a gynecologist.
What cycle length is normal?
Normal cycle length is 21-35 days (28 average). Variations of 7-9 days per year are normal. Persistent irregularity warrants medical evaluation.

Medical Disclaimer

This menstrual cycle calculator is for educational and informational purposes only. Cycle predictions are estimates based on the calendar method — they do not account for irregular cycles, PCOS, thyroid conditions, perimenopause, or other hormonal factors.

This tool is not a substitute for professional medical advice, contraception, or diagnosis. Do not use this tracker as birth control. For family planning or irregular cycle concerns, consult a gynecologist or reproductive health specialist.

If you experience severe pain, heavy bleeding, missed periods for 3+ cycles, or other concerning symptoms, seek medical attention promptly.
