❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is ovulation calculated?
Ovulation typically occurs 14 days before your next period. For a 28-day cycle, ovulation is on day 14. Our calculator uses your cycle length to predict the fertile window.
What are Ayurvedic period care tips?
Ayurveda recommends warm foods, ginger tea, sesame oil massage on the lower abdomen, and rest during periods to balance Vata dosha and reduce discomfort.
How accurate is PMS prediction?
Predictions are based on cycle phase and common symptoms reported by users of similar age groups. Individual experiences may vary.
Is my period data private?
Yes! All calculations happen in your browser. We never store, log, or transmit any of your personal health data. 100% private and anonymous.
Can I use this for contraception?
No. This tool is for cycle tracking and awareness only. It should not be used as a contraception method. Consult a doctor for medical advice.
What cycle length is normal?
Typical cycle length ranges from 21-35 days. Variations of 7-9 days per year are normal. Track consistently to understand your unique pattern.
