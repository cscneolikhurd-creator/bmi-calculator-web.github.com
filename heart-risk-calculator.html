Frequently Asked Questions

What is 10-year CVD risk?

Probability of having a heart attack or stroke within the next 10 years, based on population studies.

Why are South Asians at higher risk?

South Asians develop heart disease 5-10 years earlier with higher mortality. Our calculator multiplies risk by 1.5-2.0x.

How is heart age calculated?

Your risk compared to someone of same age/gender with optimal risk factors.

Can I improve my score?

Yes! Quit smoking, control BP, exercise daily, and follow Indian heart-healthy diet.
