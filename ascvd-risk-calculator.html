Frequently Asked Questions

What is a high ASCVD risk score in India?

Per Cardiological Society of India (CSI) 2023 guidelines, a 10-year ASCVD risk of 10% or higher is generally considered high risk and warrants statin therapy. This is more aggressive than US ACC/AHA thresholds (where 7.5% is intermediate and 20% is high).

Why is South Asian risk different?

South Asians (including Indians) develop ASCVD 5-10 years earlier than Western populations and have 2-4× higher risk at the same BMI. The ACC/AHA Pooled Cohort Equations were derived from US populations and underestimate South Asian risk by about 30-40%. This calculator allows an optional South Asian calibration (×1.4 multiplier).

What is Heart Age?

Heart Age (or Vascular Age) is the age at which a person with ideal risk factors (optimal BP, cholesterol, non-smoker, non-diabetic) would have the same 10-year ASCVD risk as you currently have. If your Heart Age is higher than your actual age, your cardiovascular system is aging faster than expected.

What are risk enhancers?

Risk enhancers increase ASCVD risk beyond what the base PCE equation predicts. Per ACC/AHA 2018: family history of premature ASCVD, chronic kidney disease, metabolic syndrome, inflammatory conditions (RA, lupus, psoriasis, HIV), triglycerides ≥175 mg/dL, hsCRP ≥2 mg/L, and Lp(a) ≥50 mg/dL.

When should I start a statin?

Per ACC/AHA 2018: Low risk (<5%) with no enhancers → lifestyle only. Borderline (5–7.5%) with ≥1 enhancer → consider moderate-intensity statin. Intermediate (7.5–20%) → moderate-intensity statin. High (≥20%) → high-intensity statin. For India (CSI), statin is generally recommended at ≥10% 10-year risk.

Is ASCVD risk the same as heart attack risk?

ASCVD risk includes heart attack, stroke, and cardiovascular death combined. It's a broader measure than just heart attack risk alone.

Can I lower my ASCVD risk?

Yes. Lifestyle changes (Mediterranean or Indian heart-healthy diet, 150 min/week exercise, smoking cessation, weight loss) and medications (statins, BP control) can significantly reduce ASCVD risk. Even a 1% absolute risk reduction is clinically meaningful.

This ASCVD Risk Calculator uses the ACC/AHA Pooled Cohort Equations (Goff DC Jr, et al. Circulation. 2014) with regional thresholds from India (CSI), UK (NICE/QRISK3), Canada (CCS), Australia (CSANZ), and Europe (ESC SCORE2). It is intended for educational and screening purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment. Risk estimates may not apply to individuals with pre-existing cardiovascular disease, severe kidney disease, or other high-risk conditions. South Asian risk calibration is an approximation and does not replace clinical judgment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting or changing any medication (including statins) or treatment plan.
