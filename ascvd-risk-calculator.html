Frequently Asked Questions

What is ASCVD risk?

ASCVD (Atherosclerotic Cardiovascular Disease) risk is the probability of experiencing a heart attack, stroke, or cardiovascular death within the next 10 years. It's calculated using factors like age, blood pressure, cholesterol, smoking, and diabetes.

What is a high ASCVD risk score?

Per ACC/AHA guidelines: Low is <5%, Borderline is 5–7.4%, Intermediate is 7.5–19.9%, and High is ≥20%. High risk usually warrants statin therapy.

Is ASCVD risk the same as heart attack risk?

ASCVD risk includes heart attack, stroke, and cardiovascular death combined. It's a broader measure than just heart attack risk alone.

Can I lower my ASCVD risk?

Yes. Lifestyle changes (Mediterranean diet, exercise, smoking cessation, weight loss) and medications (statins, blood pressure control) can significantly reduce ASCVD risk.

What is the difference between ACC/AHA and QRISK3?

ACC/AHA is the US standard based on Pooled Cohort Equations. QRISK3 is the UK standard used by NICE, which includes additional factors like ethnicity, rheumatoid arthritis, and mental health conditions.

What units does the calculator support?

The calculator supports both mg/dL (US standard) and mmol/L (international standard) for cholesterol values. Toggle the unit selector to switch.
