Frequently Asked Questions

What are macros?

Macros (macronutrients) are the three main nutrients your body needs in large amounts: protein (4 kcal/g), carbohydrates (4 kcal/g), and fats (9 kcal/g). Tracking macros is more precise than calorie counting because it ensures you get the right balance for your goals.

How much protein do I need?

For general health: 0.8-1.0g per kg bodyweight. For muscle gain or fat loss: 1.6-2.2g per kg. For athletes: up to 2.4g per kg. Higher protein preserves muscle during a calorie deficit and increases satiety.

Which diet protocol is best?

There's no single best diet — adherence matters most. Balanced (30/40/30) works for most people. High Protein (40/30/30) helps with fat loss and muscle. Keto (20/5/75) helps with epilepsy and some metabolic conditions. Mediterranean (25/45/30) has the strongest long-term health evidence.

How accurate is the BMR formula?

Mifflin-St Jeor is accurate within ±10% for most people. Individual metabolism varies based on genetics, muscle mass, thyroid function, and medications. Adjust your macros based on real-world results over 2-4 weeks.

Do I need to hit my macros exactly?

Aim for ±5g on protein and ±10g on carbs/fats. Consistency over weeks matters more than daily precision. Prioritize protein first, then fill remaining calories with carbs and fats.

When should I eat my carbs?

For most people, meal timing doesn't significantly affect results. For athletes: carbs 1-2 hours before and after workouts improve performance and recovery. Prioritize total daily intake over timing.
