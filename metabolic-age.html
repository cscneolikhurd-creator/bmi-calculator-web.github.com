Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Indians need Asian BMI cutoffs?

Indians develop metabolic disease at lower BMIs due to the "South Asian phenotype" (higher visceral fat, lower muscle mass, higher insulin resistance). WHO Asian cutoffs: normal 18.5–22.9, overweight 23–24.9, obese ≥25. The Western BMI cutoff of 25 for overweight misses metabolic risk in Indians.

How does thyroid affect BMR?

1 in 10 Indian adults has hypothyroidism. Thyroid hormones directly regulate metabolism — hypothyroidism can lower BMR by 15–40%. If you have thyroid disease, BMR formulas will underestimate metabolism. Get TSH, free T3, free T4, and anti-TPO tested. Medication (levothyroxine) normalizes BMR.

How does PCOS affect metabolic age?

PCOS affects 1 in 5 Indian women. It's associated with insulin resistance, which impairs glucose metabolism and often lowers BMR. Women with PCOS may have higher metabolic age due to hormonal imbalance. Strength training + low-GI diet (millets, dal) + weight management (5–10% loss) + metformin (if prescribed) can improve metabolic health significantly.

What is metabolic age?

Metabolic age compares your BMR to the average BMR of people your chronological age. If your metabolic age is lower than your actual age, your metabolism is more efficient than average — indicating better fitness, muscle mass, and metabolic health.

Can I lower my metabolic age?

Yes. Strength training (2-3x/week) increases muscle mass, which raises BMR. Eating adequate protein (1.6-2.2g/kg), getting 7-9 hours of sleep, staying hydrated, and managing stress all support a healthy metabolism. Results typically show in 8-12 weeks.

How accurate is metabolic age?

Metabolic age is a motivational estimate, not a clinical measurement. BMR formulas are accurate within ±10% for most people. Individual metabolism varies widely due to genetics, thyroid function, muscle mass, and medication. Use it as a trend indicator, not a diagnosis.

Can medication affect metabolic age?

Yes. Thyroid medications, beta-blockers, antidepressants, antipsychotics, and steroids can significantly affect BMR. If you're on medication that impacts metabolism, interpret results with caution and consult your doctor. Metabolic age is not diagnostic.

What is a good metabolic health score?

Our score ranges from 0-100: 80+ is excellent (metabolic age below actual age), 60-79 good, 40-59 fair, and below 40 needs attention (metabolic age above actual age). Focus on trends over time rather than the exact number.

Medical Disclaimer

This metabolic age calculator is for educational and motivational purposes only. Metabolic age is not a clinical measurement — it's an estimate based on BMR formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor / Katch-McArdle / Harris-Benedict) compared against population averages. Individual metabolic rate varies significantly based on genetics, thyroid function, muscle mass, medications, and health conditions.

This tool is not a substitute for professional medical advice, thyroid testing, or metabolic assessment. If you experience unexplained weight changes, fatigue, hair loss, or suspect a metabolic disorder (hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, diabetes, PCOS), consult an endocrinologist.

Do not change medications based on this tool's results. Always consult your healthcare provider before making health decisions.
