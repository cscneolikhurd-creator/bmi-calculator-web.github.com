❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is metabolic age?

It compares your BMR to the average for your age group. Lower is better.

How to lower metabolic age?

Build muscle, eat protein, sleep well, stay hydrated, and do HIIT.

Is it accurate?

It's an estimate based on scientific formulas. For precision, get an RMR test.
