❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is metabolic age?

Metabolic age compares your basal metabolic rate (BMR) to the average BMR of people your age. If your metabolic age is lower than your actual age, it's a sign of good metabolic health.

How can I lower my metabolic age?

Build muscle through strength training, eat enough protein, sleep 7-8 hours, stay hydrated, and do HIIT workouts. Avoid crash diets which slow metabolism.

Is metabolic age accurate?

It's an estimate based on scientific formulas. For precise measurement, consider professional testing like RMR (Resting Metabolic Rate) test.
