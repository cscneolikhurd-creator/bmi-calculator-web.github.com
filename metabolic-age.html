Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is metabolic age?
Metabolic age compares your basal metabolic rate (BMR) to the average BMR of people your chronological age. If your metabolic age is lower, your metabolism is more efficient than average.
How can I lower my metabolic age effectively?
Build muscle through strength training (3-4x/week), eat enough protein (1.6g per kg), sleep 7-8 hours, stay hydrated, and incorporate HIIT workouts. Avoid crash diets which slow metabolism.
Is the metabolic age calculator accurate?
It provides a reliable estimate based on the Mifflin-St Jeor formula and population averages. For clinical precision, consider a professional RMR (Resting Metabolic Rate) test.
What is a good metabolic health score?
A score above 70 is considered good. Scores above 85 indicate excellent metabolic health. The score combines BMR efficiency, body composition, and activity level.
Does age affect metabolism?
Metabolism naturally slows about 1-2% per decade after age 30, mainly due to muscle loss. Regular strength training can maintain or even increase metabolic rate.
