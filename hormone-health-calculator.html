Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this hormone calculator?
Based on Endocrine Society research data and Journal of Clinical Endocrinology & Metabolism. Provides estimates based on population averages and lifestyle factors — not a clinical diagnosis.
What hormones affect men vs women?
Men produce more testosterone; women produce more estrogen and progesterone. Both genders have all hormones but in different ratios. Our calculator adjusts for these differences.
How can I improve my hormone health?
Quality sleep (7-8 hours), stress management, strength training, healthy fats (avocado, nuts, olive oil), reducing alcohol, and eating whole foods are the foundations of hormone health.
What does cortisol do?
Cortisol is your primary stress hormone. High cortisol disrupts sleep, increases belly fat, suppresses immune function, and can lower testosterone or estrogen. Chronic stress is harmful.
How does age affect hormones?
Testosterone declines about 1% per year after 30. Estrogen drops sharply during menopause (typically 45-55). Cortisol and thyroid can also change with age, stress, and lifestyle.
Is my hormone data private?
Yes! All calculations happen entirely in your browser. We never store, log, or transmit any of your personal health data to any server. 100% private and anonymous.
