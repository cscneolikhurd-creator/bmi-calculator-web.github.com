    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is PCOS and why is it common in India?
    PCOS affects 1 in 5 Indian women — much higher than Western populations. Signs: irregular periods, acne, excess hair growth, belly weight gain, difficulty conceiving. Diagnosis needs 2 of 3: irregular ovulation, high androgens, polycystic ovaries on ultrasound. Blood tests: LH, FSH, testosterone, AMH + pelvic ultrasound.
    Why is thyroid disease so common in India?
    1 in 10 Indian adults has hypothyroidism. Causes: iodine deficiency, autoimmune Hashimoto's, genetics. Symptoms: fatigue, weight gain, cold intolerance, dry skin, hair loss, irregular periods. Get TSH, free T3, free T4, and anti-TPO antibodies tested annually if symptomatic.
    Why is Vitamin D important for hormones?
    Vitamin D acts as a hormone precursor affecting testosterone, estrogen, thyroid, and insulin. 70-90% of Indians are deficient despite sunshine (dark skin, indoor work, pollution, clothing). Low Vitamin D worsens PCOS, thyroid, and fertility. Target 30-50 ng/mL. Supplement as prescribed.
    Are ashwagandha and shatavari effective for hormones?
    Limited but emerging evidence. Ashwagandha (300-600 mg/day standardized) may modestly improve testosterone and reduce cortisol in small studies. Shatavari is traditionally used for female reproductive health but human evidence is limited. Do not replace medical treatment. Avoid in pregnancy. Consult your doctor before starting.
    How can I improve my hormone health?
    Quality sleep (7-9 hrs), stress management (yoga, pranayama), strength training 2-3x/week, healthy fats (ghee, nuts, seeds), adequate protein (1.6g/kg), reducing alcohol/processed foods, daily sunlight (15-20 min), and correcting Vitamin D and iron deficiencies. Avoid endocrine disruptors (BPA, parabens).
    How does age affect hormones?
    Testosterone declines ~1% per year after 30 (men). Estrogen drops sharply during menopause (45-55, women). Cortisol and thyroid can change with age, stress, and lifestyle. Regular blood tests after 40 are recommended.
    What does cortisol do?
    Cortisol is your primary stress hormone. High cortisol disrupts sleep, increases belly fat, suppresses immune function, and lowers testosterone/estrogen. Chronic stress is harmful. Managing stress is critical for hormone health.

    Medical Disclaimer

    This hormone health calculator is for educational and informational purposes only. Hormone levels cannot be accurately determined without a blood test. This tool uses questionnaire-based estimates correlated with lifestyle factors and does not replace clinical blood work (testosterone, estradiol, cortisol, TSH, T3, T4, anti-TPO).

    This tool is not a substitute for professional medical advice, endocrinologist consultation, or diagnosis of hormonal disorders (hypothyroidism, PCOS, hypogonadism, Cushing's syndrome, menopause). Do not start, stop, or change hormone therapy based on these results.

    If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, hormone levels change dramatically — consult your OB-GYN. If you experience palpitations, severe depression, or extreme fatigue, seek medical care immediately.
