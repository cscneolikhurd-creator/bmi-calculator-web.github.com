❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this?+
Based on Endocrine Society research. Provides estimates — not clinical diagnosis.
Male vs female hormones?+
Men: more testosterone. Women: more estrogen/progesterone. Both have all hormones in different ratios.
What affects cortisol?+
Cortisol = stress hormone. High cortisol disrupts sleep, increases belly fat, suppresses other hormones.
Improve hormone health?+
Quality sleep, stress management, strength training, healthy fats, reduce alcohol/processed foods.
