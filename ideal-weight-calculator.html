Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy weight for Indians?

Indians should use Asian BMI cutoffs (WHO/ICMR): Underweight <18.5, Normal 18.5–22.9, Overweight 23–24.9, Obese ≥25. For 170 cm, healthy weight is 53.5–66.2 kg. The Western "24.9" is too high — Indians develop diabetes and heart disease at lower BMIs.

Why do Indians need different cutoffs?

South Asians have the "TOFI" phenotype (Thin Outside, Fat Inside) — higher visceral fat and lower muscle mass at the same BMI. Type 2 diabetes onset occurs 5–10 years earlier, and heart disease risk rises at lower BMIs. Hence Asian cutoffs are ~2 BMI points lower than Western ones.

How do I measure frame for Indian body types?

Wrist circumference (dominant hand, below wrist bone):
Indian Men: Small <16 cm, Medium 16–18.5 cm, Large >18.5 cm
Indian Women: Small <13.5 cm, Medium 13.5–16 cm, Large >16 cm
Indians typically have smaller frames than Western populations at the same height.

What is the Peterson formula for ideal weight?

Men: 50 kg + 0.91 × (height cm − 152.4)
Women: 45.5 kg + 0.91 × (height cm − 152.4)
Formula derived from Western cohorts — use BMI range as primary guide for Indians.

Is ideal weight the same as healthy weight?

No. Ideal weight is a single number from a formula. Healthy weight is a RANGE (BMI 18.5–22.9 for Indians, 18.5–24.9 for Caucasians). Aiming for a range is more realistic — ±3–5 kg variation is still healthy.

Does body frame affect ideal weight?

Yes. Larger frames can carry more weight healthily. Formulas like Peterson don't adjust for frame — we add ±5% based on small/medium/large selection.

What is ICMR height-weight reference for Indians?

ICMR-NIN 2020 provides reference: For a 165 cm Indian man: ~55–67 kg. For a 155 cm Indian woman: ~48–59 kg. Tighter than Western ranges due to lower muscle mass and smaller frames.

How long to reach ideal weight safely?

At 0.5 kg/week, losing 10 kg takes ~20 weeks. Faster than 1 kg/week risks muscle loss and rebound. Preserve muscle with 1.6 g/kg protein + strength training while losing fat.

Medical Disclaimer

This ideal weight calculator is for educational and informational purposes only. Ideal weight formulas provide population-level estimates and do not account for individual factors (muscle mass, bone density, body composition, age, ethnicity, medical conditions).

This tool is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Athletes, pregnant women, elderly individuals, and people with metabolic conditions (diabetes, thyroid, PCOS) may have healthy weights outside standard ranges. Consult a registered dietitian or physician for personalized targets.

If experiencing unintended weight change, an eating disorder, or body image concerns, please reach out to a healthcare provider or mental health professional.
