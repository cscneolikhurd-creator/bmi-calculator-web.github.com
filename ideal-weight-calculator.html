Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Peterson formula?
The Peterson formula (2016) is the most accurate ideal weight formula, replacing older formulas like Devine and Robinson. It accounts for height more precisely.
Why Indian adjustment?
Studies show Indians have higher body fat percentage at same BMI. Ideal weight for Indians is 3-5% lower than Western standards.
How to measure frame size?
Wrap thumb and middle finger around your wrist. If fingers overlap = small frame, touch = medium, gap = large.
Healthy BMI for Indians?
For Indians, the healthy BMI range is 18.5-22.9 (lower than WHO standard of 18.5-24.9).
Is my data private?
Yes! All calculations happen in your browser. We never store any personal data.
How accurate is the projection?
Based on 7700 kcal = 1 kg fat loss. Individual results vary based on metabolism and adherence.
