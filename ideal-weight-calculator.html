Frequently Asked Questions

What is Peterson formula?

Most accurate ideal weight formula (2016), more precise than older Devine/Robinson formulas.

Why Indian adjustment?

Indians have higher body fat % at same BMI. Ideal weight is 3-5% lower than Western standards.

How to measure frame size?

Wrap thumb & middle finger around wrist. Overlap=small, touch=medium, gap=large.

How accurate is projection?

Based on 7700 kcal = 1 kg. Actual results vary by metabolism and diet adherence.
