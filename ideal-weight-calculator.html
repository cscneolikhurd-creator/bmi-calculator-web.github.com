Frequently Asked Questions

What is Peterson formula?

Most accurate ideal weight formula (2016), more precise than older formulas.

Why Indian adjustment?

Indians have higher body fat % at same BMI. Ideal weight is 3-5% lower.

How to measure frame size?

Wrap thumb & middle finger around wrist. Overlap=small, touch=medium, gap=large.
