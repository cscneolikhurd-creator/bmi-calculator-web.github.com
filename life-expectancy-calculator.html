    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    How is life expectancy calculated?

    WHO/UN life tables + Sullivan method. Adjusted for 30+ lifestyle & health factors.

    Healthspan vs Lifespan?

    Lifespan = total years. Healthspan = healthy years without chronic disease.

    How accurate is this?

    Population-level estimates. Individual genetics & future medicine not factored.

    Quick vs Deep mode?

    Quick = 9 lifestyle factors. Deep = 38 factors including blood markers.
