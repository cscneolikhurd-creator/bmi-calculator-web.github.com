Frequently Asked Questions

Why is India's life expectancy lower than Western countries?

India's life expectancy (~70.8 years, SRS 2018–22) is lower than US/UK/Canada/Australia due to: (1) air pollution (7+ million premature deaths/year), (2) high diabetes rates (101 million) with onset 10 years earlier than Western populations, (3) TB burden (highest in world), (4) anemia in women (57%), (5) maternal mortality, (6) road accidents, and (7) healthcare access gaps.

How does air pollution affect lifespan?

PM2.5 pollution is the #1 modifiable environmental risk in India. Studies show 1 year of high-exposure AQI (300+) can reduce life expectancy by 1–2 years. Delhi residents may lose 8–10 years compared to clean-air regions. Mitigation: HEPA air purifiers indoors, N95 outdoors, avoid early morning outdoor exercise on high AQI days, indoor plants, check AQI before exercise.

How much do Indian lifestyle practices help?

Evidence-based Indian practices: yoga/pranayama (blood pressure, stress), millets (fiber, glycemic control), dal + rice combining (complete protein), curd (gut health), amla (Vitamin C), haldi + kali mirch (anti-inflammatory). These support longevity but cannot override smoking, high AQI, or uncontrolled diabetes.

What is the difference between healthspan and lifespan?

Lifespan = total years lived. Healthspan = years lived in good health without chronic disease. The Sullivan method calculates this gap — most people spend 8–12 years in poor health at end of life. For Indians, the gap is often 10–15 years due to earlier onset of diabetes and heart disease.

How accurate is this calculator?

It's an educational estimate, not a medical prediction. Uses population-level WHO/UN/SRS data. Individual lifespan depends on genetics (20–30%), medical care, accidents, and factors beyond lifestyle. Use as motivation, not fate.

How can I increase my life expectancy?

Top factors: don't smoke (+7–10 years), exercise 150+ min/week (+2–3 years), sleep 7–9 hours (+1–2 years), healthy Indian diet with millets/dal/curd (+1–2 years), maintain BMI 18.5–22.9 (Asian cutoffs), manage AQI exposure, and stay socially connected.

Can lifestyle overcome bad genetics?

Yes, largely. Genetics accounts for only 20–30% of lifespan variation. Lifestyle, environment, and healthcare account for 70%+. Even with family history of diabetes or heart disease, healthy habits significantly delay onset and extend healthspan.

Is my data private?

Medical Disclaimer

This life expectancy calculator is for educational and motivational purposes only. It provides population-level estimates based on WHO/UN life tables, SRS India, and lifestyle adjustment factors. It is not a medical prediction of any individual's lifespan.

Individual lifespan depends on genetics, medical care, accidents, environmental factors, and many things beyond lifestyle. This tool is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

We deliberately do not show a predicted death date — such predictions can cause unnecessary anxiety and are medically irresponsible. Focus on the healthspan number and actionable steps you can take today.

If you have health concerns or anxiety about aging or mortality, please consult a healthcare professional or mental health counselor.
