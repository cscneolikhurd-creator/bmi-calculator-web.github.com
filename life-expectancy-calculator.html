Frequently Asked Questions

How is life expectancy calculated?

Uses WHO/UN life tables for country-specific base rates, adjusted for age, gender, BMI, smoking, alcohol, exercise, diet, sleep, and 30+ health biomarkers using the Sullivan method.

What is the difference between healthspan and lifespan?

Lifespan = total years lived. Healthspan = years lived in good health without chronic disease. The Sullivan method calculates this gap — most people spend 8-12 years in poor health at the end of life.

How accurate is this calculator?

It's an educational estimate, not a medical prediction. It uses population-level data from WHO and the UN. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, medical care, accidents, and factors beyond lifestyle. Use it as motivation, not fate.

How can I increase my life expectancy?

Top factors: don't smoke (+7-10 years), exercise 150+ min/week (+2-3 years), sleep 7-9 hours (+1-2 years), healthy diet (+1-2 years), maintain BMI 18.5-24.9, manage stress, and stay socially connected.

Can lifestyle overcome bad genetics?

Yes, largely. Genetics accounts for only 20-30% of lifespan variation. Lifestyle, environment, and healthcare account for 70%+. Even with family history of disease, healthy habits significantly reduce risk.

Is my data private?

Yes. All calculations happen entirely in your browser. We never store, log, or transmit any of your personal health data to any server. 100% private and anonymous.

Medical Disclaimer

This life expectancy calculator is for educational and motivational purposes only. It provides population-level estimates based on WHO/UN life tables and lifestyle adjustment factors. It is not a medical prediction of any individual's lifespan.

Individual lifespan depends on genetics, medical care, accidents, environmental factors, and many things beyond lifestyle. This tool is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

We deliberately do not show a predicted death date — such predictions can cause unnecessary anxiety and are medically irresponsible. Focus on the healthspan number and actionable steps you can take today.

If you have health concerns or anxiety about aging or mortality, please consult a healthcare professional or mental health counselor.
