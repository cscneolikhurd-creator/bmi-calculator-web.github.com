❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is life expectancy calculated?

Our calculator uses WHO/UN life tables for country-specific base life expectancy, then applies the Sullivan method to adjust for age, gender, BMI, smoking, alcohol, exercise, diet, sleep, and 30+ additional health biomarkers from peer-reviewed studies.

Healthspan vs Lifespan — what's the difference?

Lifespan is the total number of years you live. Healthspan is the number of years you live in good health without chronic disease or disability. The gap between them represents years of less-than-optimal health.

How accurate is this calculator?

Our estimates are based on large population cohort studies and WHO/UN data. Typical accuracy is ±3-5 years for most individuals. However, genetics, unforeseen medical advances, and environmental factors can significantly affect actual outcomes.

Quick vs Deep mode?

Quick mode uses 9 core lifestyle factors (age, gender, country, height/weight, smoking, alcohol, exercise, diet, sleep). Deep mode adds 29 additional factors including blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, stress, mental health, social support, and chronic conditions for a more personalized estimate.

What is the Sullivan method?

The Sullivan method calculates healthy life expectancy (healthspan) by subtracting the prevalence of disability and chronic disease from total life expectancy. It's the WHO-recommended standard for calculating health-adjusted life expectancy.

Is my data private?

Yes! All calculations happen entirely in your browser. We never store, log, or transmit any of your personal health data. 100% private and anonymous. No signup required.
