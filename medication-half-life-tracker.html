Frequently Asked Questions

What is medication half-life?

Half-life is the time it takes for drug concentration to reduce by 50%. It determines dosing frequency and how long a medication stays in your system. Most drugs follow first-order kinetics — 4–5 half-lives = 95–97% eliminated.

What is the half-life of common Indian medicines like Crocin/Dolo 650?

Crocin, Dolo 650, Calpol, Metacin are paracetamol brands — half-life 1.5–4 hours (avg 2–3h). Combiflam (ibuprofen + paracetamol) — ibuprofen half-life 2h, paracetamol 2–3h. Effect lasts 4–6 hours, requires dosing every 4–6 hours for fever/pain.

What is the half-life of TB drugs?

India has highest TB burden globally. TB drugs: Rifampicin (3–4h, potent liver enzyme inducer), Isoniazid (1–4h, depends on acetylator status — 50% Indians are slow acetylators), Pyrazinamide (9–10h), Ethambutol (3–4h). Standard regimen is DOTS 6–9 months. Do NOT skip doses.

How do liver/kidney disease affect half-life?

Liver metabolizes most drugs; kidney excretes them. In cirrhosis or chronic kidney disease (common in India — 17% prevalence), half-life can DOUBLE or TRIPLE. This means drug accumulation and toxicity risk. Our calculator allows liver/kidney adjustment for personalized estimates. Always inform your doctor about liver/kidney issues.

Do Ayurvedic/herbal medicines interact with drugs?

Yes, important interactions: Ashwagandha alters thyroid hormone metabolism, Tulsi/turmeric (high dose) have mild blood-thinning effect (caution with warfarin), Garcinia cambogia affects statin metabolism, Neem alters liver enzymes, Triphala can affect drug absorption. Always tell your doctor about ALL supplements.

What are emergency poison control numbers in India?

India: National Poison Information Centre (AIIMS) 1800-116-117, Emergency Ambulance 108, National Emergency 112, AIIMS Delhi Poison Control 011-26593677. For drug overdose, go to nearest hospital emergency immediately. Do NOT induce vomiting unless advised.

Can I use this to adjust my medication?

NO. This tool is for educational understanding only. Never adjust your medication dose or schedule based on this calculator. Always follow your prescriber's instructions and consult your pharmacist.

Is this calculator accurate?

Uses first-order elimination kinetics (most drugs). But: some drugs follow zero-order kinetics (alcohol, phenytoin at high doses), have active metabolites (e.g., fluoxetine), or vary widely by individual (genetics, age, weight, organ function). Use for education only.

Medical Disclaimer

This medication half-life tracker is for educational and informational purposes only. It uses first-order elimination kinetics, which applies to most drugs — but not all. Some drugs follow zero-order kinetics (alcohol, phenytoin at high doses) or have active metabolites with different half-lives.

Never use this tool to: adjust your medication dose, skip doses, or make any treatment decisions. Always follow your prescriber's instructions and consult your pharmacist or physician.

Individual half-life varies significantly based on: liver and kidney function, age, weight, genetics, drug interactions, food, and disease states. If you experience side effects or adverse reactions, contact your healthcare provider immediately.

In case of overdose: India — AIIMS Poison Control 1800-116-117 / 108 / 112. US — 1-800-222-1222 / 911. UK — 999. Go to nearest emergency room.
