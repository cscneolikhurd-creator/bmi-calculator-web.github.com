Frequently Asked Questions

What is FFMI?

FFMI (Fat-Free Mass Index) measures muscle mass relative to height. Unlike BMI, it separates muscle from fat. FFMI = Lean Mass (kg) / Height² (m²).

What is a good FFMI?

Men: 18–19 average, 20–21 above average, 22–23 excellent, 24–25 exceptional (near natural limit).
Women: 14–15 average, 16–17 above average, 18–19 excellent, 20–22 exceptional.

What is the natural FFMI limit?

Kouri et al. (1995) found drug-free bodybuilders rarely exceed adjusted FFMI of 25 (men) or 22 (women). Some genetic outliers can reach 26–27 naturally. Age, training history, and genetics all affect this ceiling — we adjust the natural limit downward by ~0.15 FFMI per decade after 30.

Can vegetarians build muscle in India?

Absolutely. Indian vegetarians have excellent protein sources: paneer (18g/100g), soya chunks (52g/100g), dal (9g/cooked cup), rajma, chole, curd, Greek yogurt, besan, peanuts, sprouts, tofu. Aim for 1.6–2.2g protein per kg bodyweight by combining dal+rice, roti+paneer, or soya+curd. Vegans need B12 supplement.

Why might South Asians have lower FFMI?

South Asians tend to have lower muscle mass and higher body fat at the same BMI compared to Caucasians — called the "South Asian phenotype" or TOFI (Thin Outside, Fat Inside). Reasons: genetics, traditional protein-light diets, and lower baseline muscle mass. Strength training + adequate protein (1.6–2.2g/kg) can close this gap significantly.

How much muscle can I gain naturally?

Beginners (0–1 yr): 0.5–1% bodyweight per month.
Intermediate (1–2 yr): 0.25–0.5%/month.
Advanced (2+ yr): 0.1–0.25%/month.
Rate slows with training age and is faster for men. A realistic FFMI gain over 3–5 years of dedicated training is 3–5 points for men, 2–3 for women.

Does age affect natural FFMI limit?

Yes. Testosterone declines ~1% per year after 30, and muscle-building capacity decreases. We adjust the natural limit downward by ~0.15 FFMI per decade over 30. A 45-year-old man's realistic limit is ~24 rather than 25. Older lifters (50+) can still build muscle but progress is slower.

Is FFMI better than BMI?

For athletes and lifters, FFMI is far better. BMI can't distinguish muscle from fat — a muscular athlete can be classified as obese by BMI. FFMI measures actual muscle mass.

Medical Disclaimer

This FFMI calculator is for educational and informational purposes only. FFMI values are estimates based on the Kouri et al. (1995) formula. Individual muscle mass varies significantly based on genetics, training history, nutrition, and hormones.

Natural FFMI limits are statistical estimates from drug-free bodybuilders, not absolute biological limits. Some genetic outliers naturally exceed these values; conversely, some lifters plateau far below. Never use this tool to accuse someone of steroid use — FFMI is not diagnostic.

Sources: Kouri EM et al. (1995) Clin J Sport Med. | Schutz Y et al. (2002) Int J Obes. | ICMR Dietary Guidelines for Indians (2024).
