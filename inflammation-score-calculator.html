Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dietary Inflammatory Index?

Scientific scoring system measuring how foods affect inflammation. Developed by University of South Carolina.

Why Indian diet adjustment?

Indian diets often include inflammatory foods (refined oils, maida, sugar). Our calculator adjusts for this.

How to reduce inflammation naturally?

Use turmeric, ginger, garlic. Replace refined oils with mustard/coconut oil. Practice yoga daily.

What is CRP?

C-Reactive Protein - a blood marker of inflammation. High CRP linked to heart disease, diabetes.
