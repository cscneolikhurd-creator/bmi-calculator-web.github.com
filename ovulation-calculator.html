Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is ovulation prediction for Indian women with PCOS?

PCOS affects 1 in 5 Indian women and causes irregular ovulation, making calendar-based prediction unreliable. If you have PCOS, use OPK (ovulation predictor kits) or consult a gynecologist for follicular tracking ultrasound. Lifestyle changes (5-10% weight loss, strength training, low-GI diet with millets) can restore regular ovulation in 60-70% of PCOS women.

How does thyroid affect ovulation?

1 in 10 Indian adults has thyroid disease. Both hypo- and hyperthyroidism can disrupt ovulation and cause irregular cycles. Get TSH, free T3, free T4, and anti-TPO tested before trying to conceive. Ideal TSH for conception is 0.5–2.5 mIU/L — much tighter than the general 'normal' range.

What is the pre-conception checklist for Indian women?

Key steps: (1) Folic acid 400–500 mcg daily starting 3 months before conception, (2) Anemia screen (57% of Indian women anemic) — take iron if deficient, (3) Vitamin D check (70-90% deficiency), (4) TSH, HbA1c, blood group, (5) Rubella immunity, (6) Dental checkup, (7) Stop smoking/alcohol, (8) Healthy BMI (Asian cutoffs).

What Indian foods boost fertility?

Fertility-friendly Indian foods: (1) Millets (bajra, jowar, ragi) — low-GI, improve insulin sensitivity (key for PCOS), (2) Ghee in moderation — healthy fats for hormones, (3) Seeds — pumpkin (zinc), flaxseed (omega-3), sesame (calcium), (4) Leafy greens — palak, methi (folate), (5) Dal + rice — complete protein, (6) Curd — probiotics, (7) Amla — Vitamin C, (8) Dates — iron, (9) Eggs — choline, B12.

How is ovulation calculated?

Ovulation typically occurs 14 days before your NEXT period, not 14 days after your last period. For a 28-day cycle, ovulation is around day 14. Our calculator uses your luteal phase length for accuracy.

What is the fertile window?

The fertile window is about 6 days: 5 days before ovulation plus the day of ovulation. Sperm can survive up to 5 days, but the egg lives only 12–24 hours.

When should I see a fertility specialist?

See a specialist if: (1) Under 35 and trying for 12+ months, (2) 35+ and trying for 6+ months, (3) Cycles irregular or >35 days, (4) Known PCOS/endometriosis/thyroid, (5) History of miscarriage, (6) Male partner has known issues. India has good fertility clinics — don't delay evaluation.

Can I use this for contraception?

NO. This tool is for cycle awareness and conception planning only. Calendar-based methods have a 12–24% typical-use failure rate. Use proper contraception.

Medical Disclaimer

This ovulation calculator is for educational and informational purposes only. Ovulation predictions are estimates based on the calendar method — they do not account for irregular cycles, PCOS, thyroid conditions, perimenopause, or other hormonal factors.

This tool is not a substitute for professional medical advice, contraception, or fertility treatment. Do not use this tracker as birth control. For conception planning or irregular cycle concerns, consult a gynecologist or reproductive endocrinologist.

If you've been trying to conceive for 12+ months (under 35) or 6+ months (35+), seek medical evaluation for potential fertility issues.

If you experience severe pelvic pain, heavy bleeding, or missed period with pain, seek immediate medical care — rule out ectopic pregnancy.
