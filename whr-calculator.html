Frequently Asked Questions

What is Waist-to-Hip Ratio?
WHR is the ratio of your waist circumference to your hip circumference. It indicates fat distribution and is a better predictor of cardiovascular risk than BMI.
What is a healthy WHR?
For women: below 0.85. For men: below 0.90. Higher ratios indicate increased risk of heart disease, diabetes, and metabolic syndrome.
Apple shape vs Pear shape?
Apple shape (high WHR) stores fat around abdomen (visceral fat), increasing metabolic risk. Pear shape (low WHR) stores fat around hips/thighs (subcutaneous fat), which is metabolically neutral or protective.
Why is WHR better than BMI?
BMI only considers weight and height, missing fat distribution. WHR specifically measures central obesity, which is strongly linked to heart disease, diabetes, and metabolic syndrome.
How to measure waist correctly?
Measure at the midpoint between the bottom of your ribs and top of your hip bone, typically at belly button level. Breath out normally and measure without squeezing.
Can WHR predict diabetes?
Yes, WHR is a strong predictor of type 2 diabetes. A WHR above 0.85 in women and 0.90 in men significantly increases diabetes risk.
