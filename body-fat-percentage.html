Tips for Healthy Body Composition

Strength Training

Incorporate resistance training 2-3 times per week to build muscle mass and increase metabolism.

Protein Intake

Consume adequate protein (0.8-1.2g per kg of body weight) to support muscle growth and repair.

Calorie Balance

Maintain a slight calorie deficit for fat loss or surplus for muscle gain, based on your goals.

Sleep Quality

Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep nightly to optimize hormone balance and recovery.

Stay Hydrated

Drink sufficient water throughout the day to support metabolism and reduce water retention.

Regular Monitoring

Track your progress consistently but focus on long-term trends rather than daily fluctuations.
