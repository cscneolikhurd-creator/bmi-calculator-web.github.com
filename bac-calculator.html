Frequently Asked Questions

What is the legal BAC limit in India?

Under Section 185 of the Motor Vehicles Act 1988, the legal blood alcohol limit in India is 30 mg of alcohol per 100 mL of blood (0.03%). This is stricter than the US (0.08%) and UK (0.08%). Penalties: First offence — imprisonment up to 6 months and/or fine up to ₹2,000. Second offence — imprisonment up to 2 years and/or fine up to ₹3,000.

How much alcohol is in an Indian peg?

An Indian peg is typically 60 ml of spirits (whisky, rum, vodka) at 42.8% ABV, containing about 20.3 grams of pure alcohol. A "large peg" is 90 ml (~30.4g) and a "small peg" is 30 ml (~10.1g). This is more than a standard US shot (44 ml at 40% = 14g).

Why is India's BAC limit lower than the US?

India's 0.03% limit is stricter because (1) Indian body composition and liver enzyme profiles (ALDH2 variants) differ from Western populations, (2) road infrastructure and traffic density make impairment more dangerous, and (3) India follows a more precautionary approach to road safety. Even 0.03% is not "safe" — ANY alcohol impairs driving.

How accurate is the Widmark formula?

The Widmark formula is accurate within ±20% for most people. It doesn't account for liver health, food intake, medications, or genetic factors. Use it for educational purposes only — never to decide whether to drive.

How long does it take to sober up?

The average person metabolizes alcohol at approximately 0.015% BAC per hour. No amount of coffee, food, or cold showers will speed this up — only time works.

What are the symptoms of alcohol poisoning?

Alcohol poisoning (medical emergency) symptoms include: confusion, vomiting, seizures, slow or irregular breathing (fewer than 8 breaths/min), blue-tinged or pale skin, low body temperature, unconsciousness, and inability to be roused. Call emergency services immediately — India: 112, US: 911, UK: 999. Do NOT leave the person alone or assume they will "sleep it off."

What is a standard drink?

A standard drink varies by country. US: 14g pure alcohol (12 oz beer @5%, 5 oz wine @12%, or 1.5 oz spirits @40%). India: ~10g (often 30 ml of 42.8% spirit). UK: 8g (1 unit). Australia: 10g. Our calculator shows both US and India standard drink counts.

Medical Disclaimer

This BAC calculator is for educational purposes only and uses the Widmark formula, which provides estimates within ±20% accuracy. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, a breathalyzer, or blood test. Individual factors (liver health, medications, food, genetics) significantly affect actual BAC. Never use this tool to decide whether to drive. If you've been drinking, don't drive — call a cab, use a ride-share, or ask a sober friend. If you suspect alcohol poisoning, call emergency services immediately (India: 112, US: 911, UK: 999).
