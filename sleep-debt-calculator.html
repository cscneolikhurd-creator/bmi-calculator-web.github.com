    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I manage sleep on Indian night shifts (IT/BPO)?

    For night shift (10 PM – 7 AM), sleep during the day in a dark room (blackout curtains + eye mask), maintain consistent schedule even on off days, avoid caffeine after 3 AM, use a white noise machine, and prioritize 7-8 hours. Fixed shifts are better than rotating. Indian IT/BPO shift-work sleep disorder is common — if you cannot sleep 1 hour after shift end, see a sleep specialist.

    Do Ayurvedic sleep aids work?

    Some have evidence: Ashwagandha (300–600 mg at night) reduces cortisol and improves sleep quality; Brahmi (300 mg) supports calm; Jatamansi is a traditional sedative. Warm milk with nutmeg has tryptophan + sedative effect. These are supportive — not replacements for medical treatment. Avoid mixing with prescription sedatives.

    Why is sleep difficult in Indian summer?

    In Indian summers (Apr–Jun), room temperatures above 30°C disrupt sleep onset and reduce deep sleep. Optimal sleep temperature is 18–22°C. Strategies: cool shower before bed, cotton/linen bedding, fan + wet towel trick, hydrate. Avoid alcohol — it worsens heat-related dehydration during sleep.

    How do I fix sleep debt from late-night OTT/WhatsApp scrolling?

    Blue light and stimulating content delay melatonin by 1–2 hours. Fix: no screens 60 min before bed, use Night Shift/warm display after 8 PM, keep phone outside the bedroom, charge overnight in another room. Add 1 hour sleep per night for 5–7 nights to repay debt.

    How do joint families affect sleep in India?

    Joint families often mean shared rooms, early-morning sounds, elder care, and no quiet space. Strategies: earplugs + white noise (fan/app), eye mask, coordinate with family on quiet hours, sleep in the coolest/quietest room, use a curtain divider if needed. Communicate openly — sleep is health, not luxury.

    What is sleep debt?

    Sleep debt is the cumulative difference between the sleep you need and what you actually get. Even 1 hour missed per night adds up to a full night's sleep by the end of the week.

    How do I recover from sleep debt?

    Recover gradually by adding 30–90 minutes of extra sleep daily over 1–2 weeks. Consistent recovery is better than weekend binge-sleeping.

    What are signs of high sleep debt?

    Signs include daytime fatigue, irritability, difficulty concentrating, increased appetite (especially sugar/carbs), weakened immunity, and slower reaction times. Chronic debt is linked to heart disease, diabetes, and depression.
