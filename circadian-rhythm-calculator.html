Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Trending Keywords Finder work?

We curate trending keywords from public sources including Google Trends, social platforms, news aggregators, and Indian government portals. Database is reviewed monthly across 5 countries and multiple categories.

Is the data real-time?

No. Curated and updated monthly, not real-time. Volumes & trends are estimates. For live data, use Google Trends directly.

Does the tool include Hinglish and regional language keywords?

Yes. We include Hinglish (Roman-script Hindi) queries such as youtube channel se paise kaise kamaye and instagram followers badhane ka tarika. Regional-language keywords (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali) can be searched by typing the native script or transliteration.

Which India-specific categories are covered?

Sarkari (SSC, UPSC, Railway, State PSC), government schemes (PM Kisan, Ayushman Bharat, Ration Card), festivals (Diwali, Holi, Navratri, Raksha Bandhan), education (NEET, JEE, CBSE, study abroad), business (freelancing, dropshipping, digital marketing), and India-specific health (PCOS, thyroid, ayurveda, yoga).

What does the intent tag mean?

Intent classifies keyword search purpose: Informational (how/what/guide), Commercial (best/vs/comparison), Transactional (buy/download/apply/register), Navigational (login/official/portal). Match your content format to the dominant intent.

What is the difficulty estimate?

Auto-estimate based on search volume + keyword length: High (high volume + short), Medium (moderate), Low (long-tail + specific). Validate against Ahrefs/Semrush for accuracy.

How can I use these keywords for SEO?

Use as starting points for content ideas. Validate with Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, or Semrush for actual search volume. Focus on long-tail keywords with clear search intent.

Is this data free to use?

Yes, 100% free. No signup required. Use for your own content research. Validate with a paid SEO tool before major content investments.

Data Accuracy Disclaimer

This Trending Keywords Finder provides curated, estimated data for educational and content-ideation purposes only. Search volumes and trend percentages are approximations. They do not represent exact search volume.

For precise keyword research, use Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush, or Moz. Always validate against your specific target audience. SEO results depend on content quality, backlinks, and technical SEO.
