Privacy Policy

We don't track you. We don't store your data. It's that simple.

Last Updated: March 19, 2026

🔐

Privacy in One Sentence

We don't collect, store, or share any of your personal data. All calculations happen entirely in your browser.

1 Introduction

At HealthCalc.in, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we handle your information when you use our health calculators and website.

The short version: We don't collect any personal data. Period.

2 Data Collection

We do NOT collect:

  • ✓ Personal information (name, email, phone, address)
  • ✓ Health data (your calculator inputs stay in your browser)
  • ✓ IP addresses (we use privacy-friendly hosting)
  • ✓ Cookies for tracking (no analytics cookies)

We use no tracking scripts, no analytics, no fingerprinting. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may have basic logs, but we don't access them.

3 How Our Calculators Work

🌐

100% Client-Side

All calculations run in YOUR browser. No data is sent to our servers.

🔒

No Storage

We don't have databases. Your data disappears when you close the tab.

📱

No Signup Required

Use all 15+ tools instantly. No accounts, no passwords.

🚫

No Third-Party

We don't share anything because we have nothing to share.

4 Cookies

We use no tracking cookies. The only cookies that might be set are:

  • Essential/Functional: None - we don't need them
  • Analytics: None - we don't track usage
  • Advertising: None - we don't show ads

Your browser may set cookies for its own functionality, but we don't control or access them.

5 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to other sites (like social media). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Always check their policies.

6 Children's Privacy

Our tools are for general audiences. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. Since we don't collect any data, this is naturally compliant.
