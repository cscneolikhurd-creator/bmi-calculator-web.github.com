Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

We don't track you. We don't store your data. It's that simple.

Last Updated: May 5, 2026

🔐

Privacy in One Sentence

We don't collect, store, or share any of your personal data. All calculations happen entirely in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.

1 Introduction

At HealthCalc.in, your privacy is our foundation. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our 25+ free health calculators. Since we collect no personal data, this policy is refreshingly simple.

The short version: We collect zero personal data. All tools work offline-capable in your browser. No signup, no tracking, no ads.

2 What We DON'T Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identifiers — Name, email, phone, address, date of birth
  • Health data — All calculator inputs (BMI, blood pressure, symptoms, etc.) stay in your browser
  • Technical data — IP addresses, device fingerprints, browser type (we use privacy-respecting hosting)
  • Cookies for tracking — No analytics, advertising, or social media tracking cookies
  • Location data — GPS, WiFi, or cell tower information
  • Behavioral data — Which calculators you use, how long you stay, what you click

We use no tracking scripts, no analytics platforms, no fingerprinting technology. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages via Cloudflare) may maintain basic server logs for security, but we do not access or use them.

3 How Our Calculators Work

🌐

100% Client-Side

All calculations run in YOUR browser using JavaScript. No data sent to any server.

🔒

No Databases

We have zero databases. Your data disappears when you close the tab.

📱

No Signup

Use all 25+ tools instantly. No accounts, no passwords, no profiles.

🚫

No Third-Party Sharing

We can't share what we don't have. Zero data = zero sharing.

4 Cookies & Local Storage

We use NO tracking cookies. The only browser storage that may occur:

  • Essential (Functional): None — our tools don't require cookies
  • Analytics: None — we don't track usage patterns
  • Advertising: None — we don't display ads
  • Local Storage: Some calculators optionally save your preferences (language, unit system) in your browser's localStorage for convenience. This data never leaves your device and is cleared when you clear browser data.

5 AI Health Tips Privacy

Some calculators offer optional AI-powered health tips using Google's Gemini API. When you request AI advice:

  • Only the specific health metric (e.g., "28% body fat") is sent to the API — no personal identifiers
  • API requests are made directly from your browser
  • We do not log, store, or review AI queries or responses
  • You can use all calculators without ever using the AI feature

6 Third-Party Links

Our website may link to external resources (research papers, health organizations). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before sharing any information.

7 Children's Privacy

Our tools are designed for general audiences (ages 13+). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. Since we collect no data from anyone, this is inherently compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar international laws.

9 Your Data Rights

Under various privacy laws, you have rights to access, correct, delete, and port your personal data. Since we hold no personal data, these rights are automatically fulfilled. If you have any concerns, contact us and we'll confirm in writing that we hold no data about you.

10 Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices (unlikely, given our architecture), we will update the "Last Updated" date. Significant changes will be announced via a notice on our homepage. Continued use after changes implies acceptance.
