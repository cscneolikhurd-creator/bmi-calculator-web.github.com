Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our top priority. We keep it simple and transparent.

Last Updated: February 2024

📋 Privacy Promise — In Simple Words

  • No signup required
  • No personal data stored
  • All calculations in your browser
  • No cookies for tracking
  • No third-party sharing
  • No ads, no trackers

1. Introduction

At HealthCalc.in, your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our health calculators and tools.

The short version: We don't collect, store, or share any personal data. All calculations happen directly in your browser.

2. What Data Do We Collect?

Absolutely nothing. Zero. Nada.

We do not collect, store, or process any personal information. When you use our calculators:

  • Your height, weight, age, and other inputs never leave your device
  • All calculations are performed locally in your browser using JavaScript
  • We don't have databases, servers, or storage systems
  • We don't use tracking cookies, fingerprinting, or analytics tools

3. How Do Our Calculators Work?

1. You Visit

You open HealthCalc.in. No account needed.

2. You Calculate

Enter numbers. JavaScript runs the formula.

3. You See Results

Results appear. Data never sent to any server.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising networks. Our website is hosted on standard infrastructure, but we have intentionally disabled all tracking features.

No Google Analytics. No Facebook Pixel. No trackers at all.

5. Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. Your browser may cache the page for faster loading, but that is controlled entirely by your browser settings and is not used by us to identify you.

6. Children's Privacy

Our tools are safe for all ages. Since we collect no data, there is no risk of collecting information from children under 13. No parental consent is needed because nothing is collected.

7. Contact Us

Have questions about our privacy practices? We're happy to answer them:

privacy@healthcalc.in
