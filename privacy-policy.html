Privacy Policy

We don't track you. We don't store your data. It's that simple.

Last Updated: June 12, 2026

🔐

Privacy in One Sentence

We don't collect, store, or share any of your personal data. All calculations happen entirely in your browser. Nothing leaves your device. Your health data stays yours — always.

1 Introduction

At HealthCalc.in, your privacy is our foundation. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our 32+ free health calculators. Since we collect no personal data, this policy is refreshingly simple and transparent.

The short version: We collect zero personal data. All tools work offline-capable in your browser. No signup, no tracking, no ads, no cookies.

2 What We DON'T Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identifiers — Name, email, phone, address, date of birth, IP address
  • Health data — All calculator inputs (BMI, blood pressure, symptoms, etc.) stay in your browser
  • Technical data — Device fingerprints, browser type, operating system (we use privacy-respecting hosting)
  • Cookies for tracking — No analytics, advertising, or social media tracking cookies
  • Location data — GPS, WiFi, or cell tower information — never requested
  • Behavioral data — Which calculators you use, how long you stay, what you click

We use no tracking scripts, no analytics platforms, no fingerprinting technology. Our hosting provider may maintain basic server logs for security, but we do not access or use them for any purpose.

3 How Our Calculators Work

🌐

100% Client-Side

All calculations run in YOUR browser using JavaScript. No data sent to any server.

🔒

We have zero databases. Your data disappears when you close the tab.

📱

Use all 32+ tools instantly. No accounts, no passwords, no profiles.

🚫

No Third-Party Sharing

We can't share what we don't have. Zero data = zero sharing.

4 Cookies & Local Storage

We use NO tracking cookies. The only browser storage that may occur:

  • Essential (Functional): None — our tools don't require cookies to function
  • Analytics: None — we don't track usage patterns
  • Advertising: None — we don't display any advertisements
  • Local Storage: Some calculators optionally save your preferences (language, unit system) in your browser's localStorage for convenience. This data never leaves your device and is cleared when you clear browser data. You can disable localStorage in your browser settings.

5 AI Health Tips Privacy

Some calculators offer optional AI-powered health tips using Google's Gemini API. When you voluntarily request AI advice:

  • Only the specific health metric (e.g., "28% body fat", "BMI 24.5") is sent to the API — no personal identifiers (name, IP, location) are included
  • API requests are made directly from your browser to Google's servers
  • We do not log, store, or review any AI queries or responses
  • You can use all calculators perfectly fine without ever using the AI feature
  • Google's privacy policy applies to API requests — View Google's Privacy Policy

6 Third-Party Links

Our website may link to external resources (research papers, health organizations, medical guidelines). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before sharing any information. These links are provided for educational purposes only.

7 Children's Privacy

Our tools are designed for general audiences (ages 13+). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. Since we collect no data from anyone, this is inherently compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar international laws. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us and we will ensure its removal.
