Frequently Asked Questions

Are HealthCalc tools free to use?

Yes, all 36 HealthCalc tools are 100% free with no signup required. We don't store any personal health data — everything runs in your browser.

Which countries does HealthCalc support?

HealthCalc supports clinical guidelines from the US (ACC/AHA, CDC), UK (NICE, NHS), Canada (CCS, Health Canada), Australia (CSANZ, ESSA), and India (CSI, API).

Is HealthCalc medically accurate?

HealthCalc uses validated medical formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor for BMR, Widmark for BAC, Pooled Cohort Equations for ASCVD, US Navy for body fat, Karvonen for heart rate zones). Tools are for educational purposes — consult your doctor for medical decisions.

How does the AI health assistant work?

Our AI assistant uses evidence-based medical knowledge to answer health questions. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice — always consult a licensed healthcare provider.

Is my health data private?

Yes. All calculations happen entirely in your browser. We never store, log, or transmit any of your personal health data to any server. 100% private and anonymous.

Do I need to create an account?

No. All tools work instantly without registration, login, or email. Your privacy is complete.
