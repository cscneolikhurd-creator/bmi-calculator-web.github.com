❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What makes your BMI calculator unique? +

Our BMI calculator shows not just your BMI, but also your visceral fat risk level, compares your BMI to others in your age group through an interactive graph, and adjusts for ethnicity (Asian, Caucasian, African) for more accurate health assessment.

How does the ideal weight calculator work? +

We use the Peterson formula combined with body frame size (small/medium/large) to give you a personalized ideal weight range. It also shows 3-month projection to reach your goal weight.

What is "Healthspan" in life expectancy calculator? +

Healthspan is the number of years you're likely to live in good health, vs total lifespan. We use the Sullivan method to show this gap and give personalized actions to improve both.

How accurate is the heart risk calculator? +

We use the Framingham Risk Score and ASCVD algorithms, which are medical-grade and used by physicians. It shows your 10-year risk of heart attack or stroke and your heart age.

How does the pregnancy due date calculator work? +

Our pregnancy calculator supports 3 methods: LMP (last menstrual period), conception date, and IVF transfer. It shows weekly fruit size comparisons, trimester info, and IOM weight gain guidelines.

What is the sleep cycle calculator? +

It calculates optimal wake-up times based on 90-minute sleep cycles. If you need to wake at a certain time, it tells you when to go to bed to wake up refreshed between cycles.

Are these calculators really free? +

Yes! 100% free forever. No signup, no credit card, no hidden fees. All calculations happen in your browser - we never store your personal data.

Can I use these on mobile? +

Absolutely! All our tools are fully responsive and work perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface automatically adjusts to your screen size.
