Frequently Asked Questions About Health Calculators

What is BMI and how is it calculated?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight. It's calculated by dividing your weight in kilograms by your height in meters squared. BMI helps categorize underweight, normal weight, overweight, and obesity.

How many calories should I eat daily?

Daily calorie needs depend on age, gender, weight, height, and activity level. Our calorie calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to provide personalized results for weight maintenance, loss, or gain.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

Healthy body fat ranges vary: For men, 6-24% is considered healthy; for women, 16-30%. Athletes typically have lower percentages. Our calculator provides an estimate based on your BMI and age.

How much water should I drink daily?

The general recommendation is 0.033 liters per kg of body weight, plus adjustments for activity level and climate. Our water calculator provides personalized hydration targets.

What is metabolic age?

Metabolic age compares your basal metabolic rate (BMR) to the average for your chronological age. A lower metabolic age indicates better metabolic health and fitness level.

How accurate are these calculators?

Our calculators use medically validated formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor, Deurenberg, etc.) and are regularly updated. However, they provide estimates - always consult healthcare professionals for medical advice.
