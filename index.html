❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What makes your BMI calculator unique? +

Our BMI calculator shows not just your BMI, but also your visceral fat risk level, compares your BMI to others in your age group through an interactive graph, and adjusts for ethnicity (Asian, Caucasian, African) for more accurate health assessment.

How accurate is the calorie calculator? +

We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which is 95% accurate for most people. Plus we show you 3 goal options (lose/maintain/gain) with macro breakdown and a 3-month weight projection chart so you can see your progress.

What is "Athlete Mode" in body fat calculator? +

Athlete mode adjusts the formula for muscular individuals who would otherwise show higher body fat due to muscle mass. It's 15% more accurate for athletes, bodybuilders, and regular gym-goers.

How does the water calculator adjust for sweat? +

Based on your self-reported sweat rate (low/average/high) and activity level, we add extra water to compensate for fluid loss. It also accounts for caffeine (diuretic effect) and climate (hot/humid weather).

Are these calculators really free? +

Yes! 100% free forever. No signup, no credit card, no hidden fees. All calculations happen in your browser - we never store your personal data.

Can I use these on mobile? +

Absolutely! All our tools are fully responsive and work perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface automatically adjusts to your screen size.
