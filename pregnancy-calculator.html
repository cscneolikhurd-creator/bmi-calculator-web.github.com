❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about due dates and pregnancy tracking.

📅 How is due date calculated?

Most commonly using Naegele's rule: LMP + 280 days (40 weeks). For IVF, we add 266 days minus embryo age.

🍎 Why compare baby to fruit?

It's a fun, visual way to track growth. For example, at week 12 baby is a lime, at week 24 a honeydew.

📊 How accurate is due date?

Only 4% of babies arrive exactly on due date. We show a 5-day window (±5 days) for 90% confidence.

👶 What if I have twins?

Multiples often arrive earlier. Select "twins" above and we adjust due date and weight gain recommendations.
