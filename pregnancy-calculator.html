❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about due dates, Garbh Sanskar, and pregnancy tracking.

📅 How is due date calculated?

Most commonly using Naegele's rule: LMP + 280 days (40 weeks). For IVF: 266 days minus embryo age at transfer. The calculator also adjusts for multiple pregnancies.

🍎 Why compare baby to Indian fruits?

Visual comparison helps moms understand baby's growth better. We use familiar Indian fruits like Nimbu, Kela, Nariyal instead of foreign fruits like cantaloupe.

📊 How accurate is the due date?

Only 4% of babies arrive exactly on their due date. We show a realistic 5-day window. The most accurate dating is via first-trimester ultrasound.

🇮🇳 What is Garbh Sanskar?

Garbh Sanskar is an ancient Indian practice from Ayurveda. It means educating the baby in the womb through healthy diet, positive thoughts, music, mantras, yoga, and reading spiritual texts during pregnancy.

🍽️ What foods should I avoid during pregnancy?

Avoid raw papaya, pineapple, raw seafood, unpasteurized milk, excessive caffeine, alcohol, and smoking. Consult your doctor for personalized advice based on your health conditions.

🔒 Is my pregnancy data private?

Yes, 100%! All calculations happen in your browser. We never store, log, or transmit any of your personal health data. No signup required.
