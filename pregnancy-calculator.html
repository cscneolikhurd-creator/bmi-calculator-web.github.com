Seek Emergency Care Immediately

Call 108 (India) or your local emergency number for any of:

  • Heavy vaginal bleeding or passage of clots
  • Severe abdominal pain or cramping
  • Severe headache with blurred vision
  • Reduced/absent fetal movements after 28 weeks
  • Fluid leakage from vagina
  • High fever (≥38°C / 100.4°F)
  • Convulsions or loss of consciousness
  • Sudden swelling of face or hands

Frequently Asked Questions

Is sex determination legal in India?

No. Under the PCPNDT Act 1994 (Pre-Conception and Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques Act), sex determination of the fetus is illegal in India. Requesting it is a punishable offense. This calculator does not and will not predict fetal sex.

What supplements do Indian pregnant women need?

Per Government of India RCH guidelines: Folic acid 400–500 mcg daily (pre-conception to 12 weeks), IFA tablets daily from 2nd trimester for 180+ days (prevent anemia), Calcium 500 mg twice daily from 14 weeks, and TT/Td vaccination (2 doses). All should be prescribed by your ANM/ASHA/doctor.

What is PMMVY (Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana)?

PMMVY provides ₹5,000 (₹6,000 for second child if girl) as partial maternity wage compensation. Apply at your nearest Anganwadi centre or PMMVY portal with MCP card, bank account, and Aadhaar. It's for first-time mothers; scheme applies for first two living children.

What is Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)?

JSY is a safe motherhood scheme under the National Health Mission that provides cash assistance to encourage institutional delivery. Benefits range from ₹600 to ₹1,400 (rural) and ₹600 to ₹1,000 (urban) depending on state and BPL status. Register with your ASHA worker at the start of pregnancy.

How much weight should an Indian woman gain during pregnancy?

ICMR recommends lower gain than IOM for Indian women because Asian BMI cutoffs are lower. For normal-BMI Indian women (BMI 18.5–22.9), recommended gain is ~10–14 kg (vs IOM's 11.5–16). Underweight (BMI <18.5): 12.5–18 kg. Overweight (BMI 23–24.9): 7–11 kg. Obese (BMI ≥25): 5–9 kg.

What are the danger signs in pregnancy?

Seek immediate medical care for: heavy vaginal bleeding, severe abdominal pain, severe headache with vision changes, high fever (≥38°C), reduced/absent fetal movements after 28 weeks, fluid leakage, convulsions, or sudden facial/hand swelling. In India, call 108 or go to the nearest government hospital.

How is the due date calculated?

Naegele's rule: add 280 days (40 weeks) to LMP. For longer/shorter cycles: EDD = LMP + 280 + (cycle_length − 28). An early ultrasound before 14 weeks is the most accurate dating method.

How accurate is the due date?

Only about 4% of babies are born exactly on their due date. About 60% are within one week, and 90% within two weeks. This is why providers call it a "due window" (37–42 weeks) rather than a "due date".
