❓ Frequently Asked Questions

📅 How is due date calculated?
Using Naegele's rule: LMP + 280 days (40 weeks). For IVF: 266 days minus embryo age.
🍎 Why compare baby to Indian fruits?
We use familiar fruits like Nimbu, Kela, Nariyal instead of foreign fruits.
🕉️ What is Garbh Sanskar?
Ancient Indian practice of nurturing the baby in womb through music, mantras, and positive thoughts.
📊 How accurate is the due date?
Only 4% of babies arrive exactly on due date. We show a 5-day realistic window.
🔒 Is my data private?
Yes! All calculations in your browser. No data stored or transmitted.
🍽️ What foods to avoid?
Raw papaya, pineapple, unpasteurized milk, excess caffeine, alcohol.
