Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is a period tracker?

Period trackers are most accurate for women with regular cycles (21-35 days). For irregular cycles, predictions become less reliable. Our tracker uses the luteal-phase method, which is more accurate than the standard 14-day ovulation assumption.

What is a normal cycle length?

A normal menstrual cycle is 21-35 days, with an average of 28 days. Cycles shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days may indicate hormonal issues and should be evaluated by a doctor.

When do I ovulate?

Ovulation typically occurs 14 days before your NEXT period, not 14 days after your last period. This is why tracking your luteal phase (usually 12-14 days) is more accurate for predicting ovulation.

What is the fertile window?

The fertile window is approximately 6 days: 5 days before ovulation plus the day of ovulation. Sperm can survive up to 5 days, but the egg only lives 12-24 hours.

Can I use this for birth control?

No. This tracker uses calendar-based predictions, which are NOT a reliable birth control method (typical use failure rate: 12-24%). Use proper contraception and consult a gynecologist for family planning.

Why is my period late?

Common causes: stress, weight changes, intense exercise, thyroid issues, PCOS, perimenopause, or pregnancy. If your period is more than 7 days late and you're sexually active, take a pregnancy test. If missed periods persist for 3+ cycles, consult a gynecologist.

Medical Disclaimer

This period tracker is for educational and informational purposes only. Cycle predictions are estimates based on the luteal-phase method and may not reflect your actual ovulation or fertile window, especially if you have irregular cycles, PCOS, thyroid conditions, or are perimenopausal.

This tool is not a substitute for professional medical advice, contraception, or diagnosis. Do not use this tracker as birth control. For family planning or irregular cycle concerns, consult a gynecologist or reproductive health specialist.
