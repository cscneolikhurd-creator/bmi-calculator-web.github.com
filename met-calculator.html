Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories does cricket burn?

Cricket (batting/bowling, casual): ~4.8 METs = ~350 kcal/hour for a 70kg person. Competitive cricket: ~6.0 METs = ~440 kcal/hour. Fast bowling: ~7-8 METs. Gully cricket is lower (~4 METs). The longer format (Test) burns less per hour than T20/ODI due to idle time.

How many calories does Surya Namaskar burn?

Surya Namaskar at fast pace: ~7.0 METs = ~520 kcal/hour for a 70kg person. Slow pace: ~4.0 METs. A typical 12-round practice (15-20 min) burns 100-150 kcal. Combines flexibility, strength, and light cardio — excellent for low-impact fitness.

What is the ICMR physical activity guideline?

ICMR-NIN 2024 recommends 60 minutes of moderate-intensity activity daily for Indian adults (higher than WHO's 150 min/week). Includes yoga, brisk walking, cycling, sports, household activities. India has high diabetes and heart disease burden — daily activity is critical.

How accurate is the MET calorie formula?

The MET formula (Calories = MET × 3.5 × weight kg / 200 × minutes) is accurate within ±10–15% for most people. Our calculator adjusts for fitness level: beginners burn ~10% more calories than trained athletes at the same work rate (real physiology — trained bodies are more efficient).

What is EPOC afterburn?

EPOC (Excess Post-exercise Oxygen Consumption) is extra calories burned after exercise to recover. High-intensity activities (HIIT, cricket fast bowling, kabaddi) produce more EPOC — typically 6–25% of total calories. Beginners see higher EPOC.

How many calories should I burn per workout?

No universal target. For fat loss, aim for 300–500 kcal per session, 3–5 times/week. For general health, ICMR recommends 60 min moderate activity daily. Focus on consistency, not maximum burn.

Why is my calorie burn different from my fitness tracker?

Fitness trackers use heart rate + accelerometer, off by 10–40%. MET-based calculations are population averages. Neither is exact. Use trends (not exact numbers) and adjust based on real-world results.

What is a MET value?

MET (Metabolic Equivalent of Task) measures energy cost of physical activity relative to rest. 1 MET = resting metabolic rate (~1 kcal/kg/hour). Walking 5 km/h is ~4.3 METs, running 10 km/h is ~9.8 METs.

Medical Disclaimer

This MET activity calculator is for educational and informational purposes only. Calorie burn estimates are based on population averages from the 2011 Compendium of Physical Activities (Ainsworth et al.) and are accurate within ±10–15% for most people. Actual calorie burn varies based on individual metabolic rate, fitness level, movement efficiency, and environmental factors.

This tool is not a substitute for professional medical advice, cardiac stress testing, or personalized fitness assessment. If you have heart disease, hypertension, diabetes, joint problems, or are pregnant, consult your physician before starting or modifying any exercise program.

Stop exercising immediately if you experience chest pain, severe shortness of breath, dizziness, or fainting. Call emergency services if symptoms persist (India: 112, US: 911, UK: 999).
