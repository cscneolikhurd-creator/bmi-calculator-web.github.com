When to Seek Emergency Care

Seek immediate medical care if you experience any of these:

  • Heavy vaginal bleeding or passage of clots
  • Severe abdominal pain or cramping
  • Severe headache with blurred vision or swelling
  • Reduced/absent fetal movements after 28 weeks
  • Fluid leakage from the vagina
  • High fever (≥38°C / 100.4°F)
  • Convulsions or loss of consciousness

India: Call 108 (ambulance) or go to nearest government hospital. International: Call local emergency number.

Indian Pregnancy Care (Govt. of India)

  • Folic acid 400–500 mcg daily — start before conception, continue till 12 weeks
  • IFA tablets daily — from 2nd trimester, 180+ days (prevent anemia)
  • Calcium 500 mg twice daily — from 14 weeks
  • TT/Td vaccination — 2 doses (as per ANM/ASHA schedule)
  • PMMVY: ₹5,000 maternity benefit — apply at Anganwadi with MCP card
  • JSY: Cash assistance for institutional delivery — register with ASHA
  • Ayushman Bharat: Free delivery + newborn care (eligible families)

Frequently Asked Questions

How is conception date calculated?

Conception typically occurs around ovulation (~14 days before your next period). From LMP: Conception = LMP + (cycle length − 14). From due date: Conception ≈ due date − 266 days. From known ovulation: due date = conception + 266 days.

What is the fertility window?

The ~6-day window when pregnancy is possible: 5 days before ovulation + ovulation day. Sperm survive up to 5 days; egg survives 12–24 hours after ovulation.

Is sex determination legal in India?

No. Under the PCPNDT Act 1994 (Pre-Conception and Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques Act), sex determination of the fetus is illegal in India. Doctors and clinics are prohibited from disclosing fetal sex, and requesting it is a punishable offense. This calculator does not and will not predict fetal sex.

What are the danger signs during pregnancy?

Seek immediate medical care for: heavy vaginal bleeding, severe abdominal pain, severe headache with vision changes, high fever, reduced/absent fetal movements after 28 weeks, fluid leakage, convulsions, or swelling of face/hands with sudden weight gain. In India call 108 (ambulance) or go to nearest hospital.

What supplements are recommended in Indian pregnancies?

Per Government of India guidelines: Folic acid 400–500 mcg daily (pre-conception to 12 weeks), IFA tablets daily from 2nd trimester for 180+ days, Calcium 500 mg twice daily from 14 weeks, and TT/Td vaccination (2 doses). All as prescribed by your ASHA/ANM or doctor.

What is PMMVY?

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana is a maternity benefit scheme providing ₹5,000 (₹6,000 for second child if girl) as partial wage compensation. Apply at your nearest Anganwadi centre or PMMVY portal with MCP card and bank details.

What is Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)?

JSY is a safe motherhood intervention under the National Health Mission, providing cash assistance to encourage institutional delivery. Benefits range from ₹600 to ₹1,400 (rural) and ₹600 to ₹1,000 (urban) depending on state and BPL status. Register with your ASHA worker.

How accurate is the due date calculation?

Only about 4% of babies are born on their exact due date. The Naegele's rule estimate (LMP + 280 days) is accurate ±2 weeks for regular 28-day cycles. Ultrasound dating in the first trimester is the most accurate. Treat the due date as a target range (37–42 weeks).
