Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Karvonen formula? +
Target HR = ((max HR − resting HR) × % intensity) + resting HR. It uses your heart rate reserve for more accurate personalized zones than the standard 220−age formula.
Which zone burns the most fat? +
Zone 2 (60–70% of max HR) uses fat as the primary fuel source. Sessions of 30–60 minutes in this zone optimize fat metabolism and are sustainable for longer workouts.
How to measure resting heart rate? +
Measure first thing in the morning before getting out of bed. Place two fingers on your wrist or neck, count beats for 60 seconds. Normal: 60–100 bpm. Well-trained athletes: 40–60 bpm.
Karvonen vs 220−age? +
The Karvonen method accounts for individual fitness via resting heart rate, making it more accurate for personalized training. The 220−age formula is simpler but doesn't consider fitness variations.
How long should I train in each zone? +
Zone 1: 5–10 min warm-up/cool-down. Zone 2: 30–60 min for fat burn. Zone 3: 20–40 min for cardio. Zone 4: 5–15 min intervals. Zone 5: 30 sec – 2 min sprints with recovery.
