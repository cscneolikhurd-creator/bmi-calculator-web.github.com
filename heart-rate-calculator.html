❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Karvonen formula?

Target HR = ((max HR - resting HR) × % intensity) + resting HR. Uses heart rate reserve for accuracy.

Which zone burns most fat?

Zone 2 (60-70% max HR) uses fat as primary fuel. 30-60 min sessions optimize fat loss.

How to measure resting HR?

First thing in morning before getting up. Count beats for 60 seconds. Normal: 60-100 bpm.

Why 5 training zones?

Each zone trains different systems: recovery (Z1), fat burn (Z2), cardio (Z3), threshold (Z4), peak (Z5).
