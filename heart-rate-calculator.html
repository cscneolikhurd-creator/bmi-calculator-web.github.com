Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Karvonen formula?
Target HR = ((max HR - resting HR) × % intensity) + resting HR. Uses heart rate reserve for accuracy.
Which zone burns the most fat?
Zone 2 (60-70% of max HR) uses fat as primary fuel. 30-60 min sessions optimize fat loss.
How to measure resting heart rate?
First thing in the morning before getting up. Count beats for 60 seconds. Normal: 60-100 bpm.
Karvonen vs 220-age?
Karvonen accounts for individual fitness via resting HR. More accurate for personalized training.
How long in each zone?
Zone 2: 30-60 min, Zone 3: 20-40 min, Zone 4-5: 1-5 min intervals.
Is my heart rate data private?
Yes! All calculations happen in your browser. 100% private.
