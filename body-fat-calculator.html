Frequently Asked Questions

What are Indian body fat standards?

Per ICMR and Diabetes Foundation India, healthy body fat for Indian men is 15–20% (overweight 20–25%, obese >25%) and for Indian women 20–30% (overweight 30–35%, obese >35%). These are stricter than ACE (US) standards because South Asians carry more visceral fat at the same BMI.

Why is Asian BMI different?

WHO Asian cutoffs are lower: Normal 18.5–22.9, Overweight 23–24.9, Obese ≥25. Indians develop metabolic disease at lower BMIs than Caucasians due to the "South Asian phenotype" — higher visceral fat, lower muscle mass, and higher insulin resistance.

What is TOFI?

TOFI = "Thin Outside, Fat Inside." It describes people with normal BMI but high body fat and visceral fat. Common in South Asians — up to 30% of normal-BMI Indians have metabolic syndrome. Our calculator flags TOFI risk based on BMI + body fat %.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

International (ACE): Men — Athletic 6–13%, Fitness 14–17%, Acceptable 18–24%, Obese 25%+. Women — Athletic 14–20%, Fitness 21–24%, Acceptable 25–31%, Obese 32%+.
Indian (ICMR): Men — Fitness 15–20%, Overweight 20–25%, Obese >25%. Women — Fitness 20–30%, Overweight 30–35%, Obese >35%.

How accurate is the US Navy body fat method?

The US Navy circumference method is accurate within ±3–4% compared to DEXA scans, when measurements are taken correctly. It's the most widely used field method for body fat estimation.

What is waist-to-height ratio (WHtR)?

WHtR = waist ÷ height. It's a better predictor of cardiometabolic risk than BMI, especially for South Asians. Healthy: <0.5. Increased: 0.5–0.6. High: >0.6. "Keep your waist to less than half your height."

What is metabolic age?

Metabolic age compares your body composition to age-group averages. If your metabolic age is higher than your actual age, you have more body fat than expected for your age. Lowering body fat and increasing muscle can reduce metabolic age by 5–10 years.

Can I reduce body fat without losing muscle?

Yes — through body recomposition: eat 1.6–2.2g protein per kg bodyweight, maintain a small calorie deficit (300–500 kcal), do resistance training 3–4x/week, prioritize sleep. Indian-friendly protein: paneer, dal, curd, eggs, chicken, soya chunks, sprouts.

Medical Disclaimer

This body fat calculator uses the US Navy circumference method, which provides estimates within ±3–4% accuracy compared to DEXA scans. Individual results vary based on measurement technique, hydration, and body composition.

Indian body fat thresholds (ICMR/DFI) and Asian BMI cutoffs are based on population-level risk data — they are guidelines, not individual diagnoses. This tool is not a substitute for professional medical advice, DEXA scan, hydrostatic weighing, or BodPod assessment. Consult a healthcare provider, nutritionist, or certified trainer for personalized goals.
