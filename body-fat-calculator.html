❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the US Navy body fat method?
The US Navy method uses circumference measurements of neck, waist, and hips (for women) to estimate body fat percentage. It's widely accepted as a reliable alternative to skinfold calipers and is within 3-4% of DEXA scan results.
What is a healthy body fat percentage for Indians?
Indians typically have 3-5% higher body fat at the same BMI compared to Western populations. Healthy ranges: Men 12-25%, Women 20-35%. Above 28% (men) or 37% (women) is considered elevated risk.
How accurate is this calculator?
The US Navy method is accurate within 3-4% of DEXA. The BMI-based method provides a quick estimate. Enable Athlete Mode if you have above-average muscle mass for better accuracy.
How can I reduce my body fat percentage?
Combine strength training with moderate cardio (150 min/week), maintain a slight calorie deficit (300-500 kcal/day), eat adequate protein (1.6g/kg body weight), and get 7-8 hours of sleep daily.
