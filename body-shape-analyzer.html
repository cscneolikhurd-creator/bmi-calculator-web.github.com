Frequently Asked Questions

What are the three somatotypes?

Endomorph: Larger bone structure, higher body fat tendency, round physique. Mesomorph: Athletic, naturally muscular, medium frame. Ectomorph: Lean, slim, difficulty gaining weight or muscle. Most people are mixed types.

How accurate is this questionnaire?

The original Heath-Carter method uses anthropometric measurements (skinfolds, bone widths, circumferences). Our questionnaire-based version is an approximation for educational guidance. It classifies you based on genetic tendencies, fat storage, muscle response, and metabolism — not a clinical measurement.

Can I change my somatotype?

Your basic bone structure and somatotype tendencies are largely genetic. However, body composition (fat and muscle) can change dramatically through diet and exercise. Consistency matters more than somatotype.

What is the best Indian diet for an endomorph?

For Indian endomorphs: focus on low-GI foods — bajra, jowar, ragi roti instead of white rice/maida; dal, sabzi, paneer, curd, eggs, chicken; avoid fried snacks, sweets, and refined carbs. Consider 16:8 intermittent fasting. Portion control is key.

What is the best Indian diet for an ectomorph?

For Indian ectomorphs trying to gain muscle: calorie surplus with ghee, nuts, dates, bananas, whole milk, paneer paratha, dal with extra ghee, rice, khichdi. Eat 5-6 meals daily. Combine with heavy compound lifts and limit cardio.

What is the best workout for a mesomorph?

Mesomorphs respond well to strength training with progressive overload. Rotate rep ranges (4-6 for strength, 8-12 for hypertrophy). Mix with moderate cardio. Rotate cutting/bulking phases every 8-12 weeks.

How common is each somatotype in India?

Most people are mixed types globally. In India, endo-mesomorph and meso-endomorph patterns are relatively common due to dietary and genetic factors, but pure types do exist. The questionnaire classifies you based on genetics, fat tendency, muscle response, and metabolism.

Can somatotype predict my fitness results?

Somatotype gives general tendencies, not destiny. Ectomorphs may find muscle gain harder but can succeed with calorie surplus + heavy lifting. Endomorphs may store fat easily but can build strength quickly. Consistency, nutrition, and training matter more than somatotype.

Medical Disclaimer

This body shape analyzer uses a questionnaire-based approximation inspired by the Heath-Carter somatotype method. The original Heath-Carter method uses anthropometric measurements (skinfolds, bone widths, circumferences) for classification. Our tool provides educational guidance only.

Somatotype is largely genetic and cannot be changed significantly. However, body composition (fat and muscle) can be improved through diet and exercise. This tool is not a substitute for professional fitness assessment, DEXA scan, or medical advice.

Sources: Heath BH, Carter JEL (1967) Somatotyping, Carter JEL (2002) The Heath-Carter Anthropometric Somatotype, ACSM Guidelines, ICMR Dietary Guidelines for Indians.
