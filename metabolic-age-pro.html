❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is metabolic age?
Metabolic age compares your BMR to the average BMR of people your age. If your metabolic age is lower than your actual age, your metabolism is better than average.
Katch-McArdle vs Mifflin-St Jeor?
Katch-McArdle is more accurate if you know your body fat % because it uses lean body mass. Mifflin-St Jeor is best if body fat is unknown.
How can I improve metabolic age?
Build muscle through strength training, eat enough protein, stay hydrated, get quality sleep, and do HIIT cardio.
What is a good metabolic age?
A metabolic age equal to or lower than your chronological age is considered good. The lower, the better.
