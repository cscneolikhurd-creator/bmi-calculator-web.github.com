Frequently Asked Questions

What is metabolic age?
Metabolic age compares your basal metabolic rate (BMR) to the average BMR of people your chronological age. If your metabolic age is lower, your metabolism is more efficient than average.
Katch-McArdle vs Mifflin-St Jeor?
Katch-McArdle is more accurate if you know your body fat percentage because it uses lean body mass. Mifflin-St Jeor is a reliable alternative when body fat is unknown.
How to improve metabolic age?
Build muscle through strength training (3-4x/week), eat adequate protein (1.6-2.2g per kg lean mass), get 7-9 hours of sleep, stay hydrated, and incorporate HIIT workouts 2x/week.
What is a good metabolic age?
A metabolic age equal to or lower than your chronological age is considered good. The lower the number, the more efficient your metabolism generally is.
How does body fat affect metabolic age?
Higher body fat lowers lean mass, which reduces BMR and increases metabolic age. Reducing body fat while maintaining muscle improves metabolic age significantly.
