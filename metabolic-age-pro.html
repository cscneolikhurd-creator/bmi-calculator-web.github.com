❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is metabolic age?
It compares your BMR to the average BMR of your age group. Lower means better metabolism.
Katch-McArdle vs Mifflin-St Jeor?
Katch-McArdle uses lean mass (more accurate). Mifflin-St Jeor uses weight/height.
How to improve metabolic age?
Build muscle, eat protein, stay hydrated, sleep well, and do HIIT.
What is a good metabolic age?
Equal to or lower than your chronological age is considered good.
