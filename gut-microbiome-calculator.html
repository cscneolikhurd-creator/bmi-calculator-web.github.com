❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is gut microbiome diversity?+
Higher diversity means more types of beneficial bacteria, linked to better health. The American Gut Project found eating 30+ different plant foods weekly leads to the most diverse microbiomes.
How accurate is this calculator?+
Based on peer-reviewed research from Nature Reviews, American Gut Project, and Harvard. It provides a research-based estimate — not a clinical diagnosis.
Probiotics vs Prebiotics?+
Probiotics are beneficial bacteria (yogurt, kefir). Prebiotics feed those bacteria (garlic, onions, fiber). You need both for optimal gut health.
How to improve my score?+
Eat 30+ plant foods weekly, include fermented foods daily, reduce processed foods, manage stress, sleep 7-8 hours, and exercise regularly.
