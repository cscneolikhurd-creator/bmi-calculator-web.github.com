Frequently Asked Questions

What is gut microbiome diversity?+
Higher diversity means more types of beneficial bacteria, linked to better overall health. The American Gut Project found that eating 30+ different plant foods weekly leads to the most diverse and healthy microbiomes.
How accurate is this calculator?+
Our algorithm is based on peer-reviewed research from Nature Reviews, the American Gut Project, and Harvard Medical School. It provides a research-based estimate of gut health — not a clinical diagnosis.
Probiotics vs Prebiotics?+
Probiotics are beneficial bacteria found in fermented foods (yogurt, kefir, kimchi). Prebiotics are fiber-rich foods that feed those bacteria (garlic, onions, bananas, oats). You need both for optimal gut health.
How to improve my gut health score?+
Eat 30+ different plant foods weekly, include fermented foods daily, reduce processed foods and sugar, manage stress through meditation, get 7-8 hours of sleep, and exercise regularly.
Is my gut health data private?+
Yes! All calculations happen entirely in your browser. We never store, log, or transmit any of your personal health data to any server. 100% private and anonymous.
How long to see improvements?+
Gut microbiome changes can begin within 24-48 hours of dietary changes. Significant improvements in diversity typically take 2-4 weeks of consistent healthy eating and lifestyle changes.
